FAQ.

The five questions every operator asks first. Longer answers and more questions live on /services/#faq.

Do you build for restaurants outside the DMV?

Yes. The cities pages exist because in-person discovery is the default for the DMV; remote-first works fine for everyone else. Same posted pricing, same calendar discipline.

What if I just want my menu page rebuilt, not the whole site?

That’s exactly what the $1,500 menu drop-in is for. Seven days, plugs into your existing site, no rebuild required.

Do you offer free discovery calls?

Yes — a 20-minute call through the Window or directly at /studio/call/. I’d rather have you read /services/ first so the call covers your specifics, not the boilerplate.

Who owns the website when you’re done?

You. Domain in your name. DNS in your registrar. Codebase in a Git repo you own. Stripe account is yours. The day you decide to leave, you take everything — no proprietary CMS, no platform tax. /never/ #1.

What’s your turnaround if my project is urgent?

Two builds run at any given time, no exceptions. If the calendar can’t carry your project, you hear so on day one — with a date you’d be next, or a referral to a peer studio that can. /never/ #5.
