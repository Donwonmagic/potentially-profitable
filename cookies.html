What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember information about you and your preferences, making your experience better on repeat visits.

There are two main types of cookies: session cookies, which expire when you close your browser, and persistent cookies, which remain on your device for a longer period. Cookies can be set by the website you're visiting (first-party cookies) or by third-party services that the site uses.

How to Control Cookies

You have control over cookie settings in your browser. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or alert you when a cookie is being set. You can also delete cookies that have already been set.

Here are links to cookie control settings for major browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Apple Safari
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Microsoft Edge

Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of our website. Some features may not work as intended if cookies are blocked.

Impact of Disabling Cookies

If you choose to disable cookies, you should be aware of the following potential impacts:

  • You may not be able to use all features of our website.
  • Your experience may be less personalized, and you may see repeated content or have to re-enter information.
  • Analytics on this site do not use cookies, so disabling cookies has no effect on analytics.
  • Third-party services like scheduling tools may not function properly.

Essential cookies cannot be disabled, as they're required for the website to function.
