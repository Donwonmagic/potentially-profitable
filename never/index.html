The studio · standing commitments

Five things I will never do.

Restaurant operators have been burned by enough vendors to be cautious by default. Fair. This page exists so you don’t have to ask. If any of these change, this page changes first — and the old version stays in git history.

No. 1

I will never lock you in.

On full payment, you own every line of code, every asset, every domain, every login. No proprietary CMS. No multi-year contract. No "platform fee." If you want to take the site to another developer tomorrow, I will hand them the repo and the runbook the same day.

No. 2

I will never resell your data.

Not your email. Not your numbers. Not your invoice line-items. Not your menu changes. Not your reservation logs. Not in aggregate, not anonymized, not "for product improvement." The free tools run in your browser; the paid work stays between us. The security page documents the receipts.

No. 3

I will never hide pricing behind a call.

Essentials from $2,500. Full Service $5–9k. Restaurant Group $8–15k+. Care Plan $125–$350/mo. Posted, not negotiated. See the full pricing. If you can’t price-compare a vendor’s work in three minutes from their website, you’re the product.

No. 4

I will never run a remarketing pixel on the library.

The library is free, no signup, no email gate. There is no Meta pixel, no LinkedIn Insight tag, no Google Ads conversion tracker, no third-party retargeting. Read an article, run a tool, leave — nothing follows you. Receipts here; verify in DevTools → Network.

No. 5

I will never take work I can’t ship in the quoted window.

Two builds active at a time. Essentials in 2–3 weeks. Full Service 4–8. Restaurant Group 6–10. If the calendar is full, you’ll get a date or a referral — not a maybe. The honest answer beats the optimistic one every time.

Hold me to it.

These are commitments, not slogans. If you ever see Muntin Digital break one of them, write to me — I will fix it, document the fix on /changelog/, and tell you what changed.
