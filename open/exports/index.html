Every number on this page is the nominal dollar value of the tracked export record — a within-record measure of what was shipped, mixing quantity and price. It is never import/export volume (not published at HS6), never a delivered or retail price, and never a forecast. Counter-seasonal export/import overlap is co-occurrence in the calendar, never a claim of cause.
