The Feed Complex

Walk a growing season with the two crops that stand behind the feed cost of nearly every animal-protein and dairy price — corn and soybeans. Each week's good-to-excellent share is read only against its own ~40 years of same-week history: an observed present-state rating, never a forecast of yield or price, and never a per-ingredient driver.

This is a coincident backdrop on the feed complex — the observed health of the corn and soybean crop. It is not a driver of any single tracked food price, carries no ingredient reference, and forecasts nothing. A rising percentile describes the historical record, never what comes next.
