Farm-gate price is the price the grower receives at the farm — it is not a delivered, wholesale, or retail price, and the two differ by freight and markup. Net trade = export value − import value (nominal customs value, mixes price and quantity).

Source. USDA National Agricultural Statistics Service (NASS) — production value, farm-gate price received, yield, and harvested area, a US-Government work in the public domain. Joined to US Census Bureau international-trade value (public domain) for the apparent-consumption import-reliance proxy. Honesty. Farm-gate price is grower-received, never the wholesale reference or a delivered price. Trade figures are nominal value, never volume (volume is unpublished at HS6). Import reliance is a cross-source apparent-consumption proxy, descriptive of supply shape — never a risk forecast, and co-occurrence is never cause. Price-received series silently mix classes and units, so any first-vs-last percentage across a raw series can be a unit artifact; the single farm-gate slice shown per ingredient is the honest read.
