The Cut and the Chain

Walk the price of meat from farm to wholesale to grocery retail — beef, pork, broiler, monthly since 1970, in cents per pound of retail equivalent. The reveal: almost the entire farm-to-retail markup lives in the wholesale→retail leg — downstream of where a kitchen buys. Retail here is USDA ERS national-average grocery retail, never a delivered, invoice, or menu price.

"Net farm value" and the spreads are ERS accounting series, not individual transaction prices. Figures are descriptive of the tracked series, never a forecast; the May 2020 wholesale spike is shown with a described coincident backdrop, never a causal claim.
