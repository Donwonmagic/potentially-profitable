The Energy Backdrop

The cost of the energy the food system runs on — diesel under freight, natural gas under greenhouse heat, electricity under refrigeration. Each carrier is read only against its own multi-decade range, so three incommensurable units become comparable. A coincident, site-wide backdrop — never a driver of any one ingredient's price, and never a forecast.
