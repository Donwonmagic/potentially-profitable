The Origin Climate Envelope

The produce a US kitchen depends on grows somewhere US drought and freeze maps can't see. This reads observed weather — never a forecast — at a representative station in each import origin's growing belt, drawn as a 12-month climatological band (1991–2020) with the most-recent readings' departure from that normal laid over the top. A regional proxy, held-until-calibrated: a station is not the farm.

The shape is the exposure

Two belts, two bands. Read which origin dips below the chill line and which never does — at a glance, with zero forecasting.

The thickness of the band is the diurnal range — the belt's climate fingerprint. Michoacan's wide band sags to the chill line at the depth of winter; Costa Rica's thin ribbon floats high and never touches it. Then the recent departure comb fades in: warm rust marks lifting off the winter floor across the grid at once — the winter floor rising inside the record itself.

Bands are 1991–2020 monthly normals; comb marks are each station's own most-recent readings vs that month's normal. Descriptive of the tracked record, not a forecast.

The origin climate grid

One card per growing belt, foods labeled. Filled band = normal TMIN floor to TMAX ceiling, Jan→Dec. The dashed chill line sits at the same temperature on every card, so you can see which winter floors dip below it.

Current thermal tilt, all belts at once

One lane per station. Each mark is a recent night-temperature (TMIN) departure, zero-centered. Rust ▲ = warmer than its own normal, teal ▼ = cooler. Descriptive only — no belt is implied to cause another, and no line is projected past the last real dot.

* recent-departure window spans far more than a ~45-day stretch (sparse daily reporting) — read that mean with a wider error bar than the dense stations. † monthly normals built from a thin baseline sample; indicative, not robust. Both are disclosed per station in the source table below and the provenance drawers.

About this data

Source: NOAA NCEI Global Historical Climatology Network — Daily (GHCN-Daily), per-station TMIN/TMAX/PRCP. US-government public domain, redistributable. Values arrive in tenths of °C; divided by 10 here.

Method: monthly climatological normals over the 1991–2020 baseline, computed with a robust mean that drops physically-impossible readings (e.g. a single −24.7 °C TMIN in coastal Sinaloa whose true winter floor is ~14 °C). Recent departure = each station's last 45 valid daily readings minus the corresponding month normal. Observed weather, never a forecast; a regional proxy for the origin belt, not the exact farm — held-until-calibrated. Crop→region assignments follow data/ingredient-weather-codes.json.
