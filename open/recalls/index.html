What this can't tell you

  • Notices are not events. The notice count is lot-level paperwork; a single situation can be dozens of notices. Lead with distinct events.
  • Never sum across ingredients. 718 recalls carry 1026 ingredient tags — one recall can name several ingredients.
  • Variant slugs triple-count. shrimp / shrimp-pd / shrimp-head-on are the same product family listed under separate tracked slugs.
  • The matcher is text, not sourcing. A whole-word product-text match pulls in composite and store-brand products that merely name the ingredient — the tag is not a supply or price link.
  • FDA jurisdiction only. These are FDA-regulated foods only; USDA/FSIS meat, poultry and egg recalls are structurally absent, so the lane is not complete.
  • A record, not a forecast. Every row is a documented past action; nothing here predicts a future recall — and nothing here is a price.
