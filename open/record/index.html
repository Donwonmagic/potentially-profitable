Rights & honesty. The Muntin corpus columns (posture, band, yields, trim) are CC BY 4.0 (Muntin Cost Index); US Census general imports — public domain (US Government work). Import/export figures are nominal customs value (they mix price and quantity), never a delivered or retail price. The seasonal index is a within-year ratio (1.0 = a typical month), inflation-immune. Source shares describe supply diversity, not a shortage forecast. Reliance is an apparent-consumption proxy. Co-movement is co-occurrence inside a six-week window — never a causal arrow. Absent layers are shown as an explicit “no record” cell, never zero-padded.
