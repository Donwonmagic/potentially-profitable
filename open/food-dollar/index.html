The Food Dollar

Follow a single US food dollar as it splits across the marketing chain. Of every dollar Americans spent on domestically-produced food in 2023, about 15.9¢ is farm share and 9.1¢ is farm value-added — two honest answers to “how much reaches the farm.” The rest is processing, retail, restaurants, and the services that move food to the plate. A national macro statistic across 31 years — never a per-ingredient price, never a forecast.

Farm share and farm value-added measure the same farm two ways; both are reported here so neither can be quoted alone. Co-movements shown (e.g. foodservices vs farm share) are associations across the record, never asserted as cause.
