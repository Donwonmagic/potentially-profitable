Features

Everything a restaurant website needs —
and nothing it doesn't.

Every build starts with the same six things. We only add complexity when your restaurant actually needs it.

Menus

Menus that update in 30 seconds

No more PDFs. No more calling your nephew. Update a price, add a special, or swap a dish from your phone in under a minute — and the site re-renders before you close the tab.

Reservations

Reservations that come to your inbox

OpenTable, Resy, Tock, or a lightweight built-in form that emails you and your hostess stand — your choice. We wire up whatever your existing workflow already trusts.

Ordering

Online ordering that doesn't eat your margin

Toast, Square, ChowNow, or a direct checkout for pickup — integrated so a hungry visitor can go from your menu to checkout in three taps without leaving your brand.

Local SEO

Google Business Profile that works

Most restaurant traffic comes from Google Maps, not organic search. We set up and optimize your Google Business Profile so you show up in the local pack when someone nearby Googles "best [your cuisine] near me."

Performance

Fast mobile pages, under 2 seconds

Your customer is standing on a sidewalk, hungry, on 4G. If your homepage takes four seconds to load, you lost them. Every Muntin build hits sub-2-second mobile load out of the box — and we measure it.

Photography

Photos that make people hungry

A responsive gallery that treats your food, space, and crew the way they deserve. Bring your own photographer or we'll recommend one in the DMV — either way, the site is built to show them off.
