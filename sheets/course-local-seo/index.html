Common mistakes

What operators get wrong

  • Picking phrases that have your restaurant name in them. Diners who know your name already type it.
  • Skipping the neighborhood specificity. 'Silver Spring' is too broad; 'Silver Spring centro' is the win.
  • Treating SEO as a one-time setup. The L16 rhythm catches the drift.
