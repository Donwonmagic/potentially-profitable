When to use it

Pull this sheet out when —

  • Last week of every month — plan the next 30 days before the urgent eats the important.
  • Around any holiday or event — plan content alongside the holiday-hours-planner so the messages match.
  • After a menu change — content needs to lead the swap, not lag it.
Common mistakes

What operators get wrong

  • Posting only food. Mix in people, place, and process — guests follow restaurants for the room as much as the plate.
  • No CTA on a single post. Every post answers "what now?" — book, order, follow, or share.
  • Scheduling without dates. "Coming soon" is not a calendar; a date and channel are.
