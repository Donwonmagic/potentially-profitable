When to use it

Pull this sheet out when —

  • Right after claiming a new GBP — work the full list before going live.
  • Monthly per the L16 hours-check rhythm — re-verify name/address/phone/hours.
  • After any operations change that affects what's true on the GBP.
Common mistakes

What operators get wrong

  • Claiming the GBP and then leaving the categories at the default 'Restaurant.' Pick the most specific category Google offers.
  • Skipping the attributes section ('outdoor seating,' 'wheelchair accessible,' 'serves vegetarian'). These drive filtered searches.
  • Letting Google's auto-suggested edits go unmoderated. They show up in your dashboard; review weekly.
