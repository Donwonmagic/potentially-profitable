When to use it

Pull this sheet out when —

  • Quarterly review — find the asset that disappeared and the license about to lapse.
  • Onboarding a new designer or marketing hire — they get the inventory before they get the work.
  • Before any rebrand — you cannot redesign what you cannot find.
Common mistakes

What operators get wrong

  • Logos stored only on a former employee's laptop. Make the cloud the source of truth, not the heirloom.
  • No license records. A stock-photo contract that lapsed is a lawsuit waiting to happen.
  • Mixing working files with deliverables. The .ai source and the exported PNG are different artifacts; track both.
