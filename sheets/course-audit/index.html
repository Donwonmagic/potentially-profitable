Common mistakes

What operators get wrong

  • Skipping the phone test. Every audit must be done on a phone first.
  • Treating cosmetic issues as severity-1. Stale hours and broken phone numbers win.
  • Auditing as the owner, not as a diner. Look up your own restaurant in Google.
