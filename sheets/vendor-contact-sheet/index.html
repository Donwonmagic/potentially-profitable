When to use it

Pull this sheet out when —

  • When the dishwasher breaks at 7pm on a Saturday and you need the equipment vendor's after-hours number.
  • Quarterly review — call each contact, verify they still work there, update the sheet.
  • Onboarding any new manager — they get the list before they get a key.
Common mistakes

What operators get wrong

  • Out-of-date sales-rep names. People move; the account number stays. Verify quarterly.
  • Missing the after-hours line. The 9-to-5 line is great until it isn't 9-to-5.
  • Storing it digital-only. The phone is dead, the WiFi is out — the printed sheet by the office phone is the safety net.
