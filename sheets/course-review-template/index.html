Common mistakes

What operators get wrong

  • Responding from defensiveness. The template is name-own-repair, not explain-deflect-promote.
  • Cutting and pasting the same response across reviews. Diners read the responses too.
  • Letting bad reviews sit longer than 24 hours. A week-late response reads as panic, not care.
