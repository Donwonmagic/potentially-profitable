Most independent restaurant websites lose money in one of three places.
After running the free audit on a few hundred sites in the DMV, the
breakdown looks like this:

  • 41% — Hours buried under a hero image. Phones spend a second loading
          a 2 MB photo before the visitor can confirm whether you're
          open. Some don't wait.

  • 27% — Menu lives as a PDF. People on phones can't pinch-zoom that
          without losing context. They bounce to your aggregator listing,
          which costs you 30% of the order they place there.

  • 18% — Reservation button doesn't deeplink. It opens OpenTable's
          home page instead of your venue's page. One click extra is
          one reservation lost.

  • 14% — Everything else combined: schema gaps, slow LCP, no Spanish
          version, contact form that emails a noreply@gmail.com that
          nobody reads.
