I'll keep this one short.

A guest who shows up to your restaurant on a Sunday — a Sunday you
told the internet you were open — and finds the door locked, is the
most expensive bug in your tech stack. They don't come back. They
write the review.

The fix is boring: someone has to verify, every month, that the hours
on your site, your Google Business Profile, your Yelp listing, and
your Apple Maps card all match the hours your kitchen actually keeps.
And someone has to flag holiday hours fourteen days out, before
Thanksgiving / Christmas / Valentine's / Mother's Day.

If you've got a manager who already does this, great. Skip this email.

If you don't, the free Holiday Hours tool builds the schema, the copy,
and the GBP paste in about two minutes — and the Store Hours tool keeps
the whole grid in one place you can re-run before every holiday:

  https://muntin.digital/tools/store-hours/

(The old $99/mo care plan retired with the services business. The tool
is the part of it you can run yourself, free, in less time than the
email asking me to do it.)

The boring stuff is what saves the next bad review.
