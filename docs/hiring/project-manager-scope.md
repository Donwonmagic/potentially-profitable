# First hire — Project Manager scope

The first non-Don role at Muntin Digital. Hires the productization layer
introduced in Phase 2, not the build layer. Don keeps writing, designing,
and shipping; the PM handles every interaction from "send a one-line
note" to "the audit PDF lands in your inbox" to "the Care Plan Light
monthly hygiene pass is done."

## Why now (the trigger)

The first hire is gated on **all three** of:

  1. ≥10 active Care Plan Light subscribers ($990/mo MRR floor).
  2. ≥2 paid $499 audits per month, sustained 60 days.
  3. Don's calendar reports >15 hours/week on operations Don shouldn't
     be doing — intake routing, audit scheduling, monthly hygiene runs,
     invoice followups.

Hire below those thresholds and the PM doesn't have enough volume to
justify the cost; hire above, and Don is the bottleneck that prevents
Phase 3 client growth.

## What the PM owns

Day one, in writing, with SOPs in `/docs/sops/` (TBD):

  • **Intake routing.** Every /window/ submission inside 2 business
    hours: triage, draft Don's 2-line acknowledgement, queue the
    studio response with a draft.
  • **$499 audit delivery.** Schedule the recording slot, run the
    pre-audit checklist on the client's URL, prep Don's screen-share
    setup, package the Loom + 8-page PDF action sheet, send the
    invoice and the deliverable, schedule the 30-day check-in.
  • **Care Plan Light monthly hygiene pass.** Hours/holiday-hours/menu-
    price verification across site + GBP + Yelp + Apple Maps, for every
    subscriber. Flag mismatches to Don for client outreach. Send the
    monthly note (templated; PM customizes the "what we caught this
    month" section).
  • **Stripe ops.** Subscription churn, failed-card recovery, monthly
    revenue summary to Don.
  • **Calendar discipline.** Don's project calendar (two active builds,
    no exceptions). Push back on Don when he says yes to a third.
  • **Newsletter scheduling.** Library Letter and the Restaurant
    Operator Drip — staging the next message in Buttondown, tracking
    the `intent_source` segments, archiving replies for Don.

## What the PM does NOT own

Hard line. If any of these creep into the PM's role, the hire is wrong
or Don is misusing the hire:

  • Writing on the library or the blog. (Voice contract per
    `/methods/#voice-contract`.)
  • Quoting a project. Quotes are Don's signature.
  • Designing or building. The studio is one operator-built voice.
  • Talking to clients without Don in the loop on substance.

## Compensation

  • $32–38/hour, contractor, 1099. Capped at 25 hours/week initially.
  • Quarterly review: scope expansion, hourly bump, or W-2 conversion.
  • No equity. The studio stays a Maryland LLC.

## Profile

The right person is, in order:

  1. **Restaurant operator-adjacent.** Front-of-house manager,
     beverage director, GM, or someone who's run a kitchen. Not a
     marketing agency project manager. Vocabulary matters; the PM
     interfaces with chefs and owners.
  2. **A decent writer.** Will draft Don's two-line acknowledgements
     and the monthly hygiene notes. Does not need to be Don.
  3. **Comfortable with light tooling.** Stripe dashboard, Buttondown,
     Loom, Google Search Console. Will not need to write code, but
     needs to read a CSV and not flinch at the GBP API console.
  4. **Bilingual EN/ES — strong preference.** A meaningful share of
     the inbound is from bilingual operators or operators serving
     bilingual neighborhoods. Native or fully-fluent Spanish makes
     the Care Plan hygiene pass twice as fast.
  5. **DMV-local.** Not a hard requirement, but the studio's
     credibility is local. In-person discovery and Bethesda /
     Silver Spring / Takoma Park / DC restaurant-floor familiarity
     matter.

## Onboarding plan (week 1–4)

Week 1:
  - Shadow Don through three full /window/ inbound responses.
  - Read /never/, /ai/, /methods/, /security/ end-to-end.
  - Run the free audit on five DMV restaurant sites and write up
    what they'd see in a paid audit. Don edits.

Week 2:
  - Run the next inbound batch with Don in the room.
  - First Care Plan Light hygiene pass on three subscribers, with
    Don over the shoulder. Discrepancies flagged in writing.

Week 3:
  - Schedule and run a paid audit end-to-end with Don in the
    pre-meeting and post-call wrap.
  - Send the first monthly note (template Don's draft, PM customizes
    findings).

Week 4:
  - Inbound triage independently, Don confirms substance only.
  - First independent audit packaging.
  - Quarterly check-in scheduled.

## Termination clause

Both directions. Two weeks written notice, no penalty either way.
Buyback / re-engagement is fine and historically the norm.
