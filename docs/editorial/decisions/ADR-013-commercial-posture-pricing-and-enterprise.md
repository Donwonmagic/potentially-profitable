# ADR-013 — Commercial posture: per-location pricing; enterprise parked post-GA

- **Status:** Accepted (founder decisions 2026-07-09, in session)
- **Date:** 2026-07-09
- **Owner:** Strategic council (founder-signed)
- **Review by:** post-GA (2026-11-13) or on founding-list demand evidence, whichever first
- **Relates to:** `data/sourced-claims.json#ledger_founding_offer_2026` (the registered
  claim); `/ledger/` + `/es/ledger/` pricing surfaces; the ledger demo's price lockup;
  ADR-003 (one-person voice — no fake-team enterprise theater).

> Decision 1: the Muntin Ledger founding rate is **$19/month PER LOCATION** ("$19 al
> mes por local"). Registered in the claim (basis founder-confirmed 2026-07-09) and
> applied to every surface quoting the terms: /ledger/ (lead, price-terms, JSON-LD),
> homepage stance, the demo lockup + founding sentence + micro-ask, EN+ES.

> Decision 2: **no enterprise tier now.** Enterprise is PARKED as a post-GA decision,
> gated on real founding-list demand evidence. Until then, the per-location posted
> rate IS the group offer (N locations = N × $19, posted in writing, no sales call).

## Rationale (council counsel, founder-accepted)

- Per-location posted pricing already serves multi-unit groups legibly and honestly —
  the demo review's 4-location ops buyer can bring "4 × $19" to an owner today.
- Enterprise machinery (negotiated pricing, procurement, SLAs, SSO) is founder-time —
  the scarcest resource — and "contact us" pricing contradicts the posted-pricing
  covenant that is the moat.
- The founding list only became able to grow on 2026-07-06 (confirm-send fix); demand
  evidence should drive the enterprise question, not speculation.

## Revisit triggers

- Multi-unit groups (≥5 locations) joining the founding list or replying to the
  dispatch asking for consolidated terms.
- Post-GA revenue stable enough to fund founder-time on sales machinery.
- If revisited: any enterprise offer must keep pricing posted in writing.
