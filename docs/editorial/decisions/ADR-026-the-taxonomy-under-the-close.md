# ADR-026 — Nine topics become four: the taxonomy is what a crawler reads first

**Status:** Proposed — blocked on a green deploy (queue Q-005)
**Date:** 2026-08-07
**Owner:** Don Goldstein

> **Decision.** `data/topics.json` drops from nine topics to four —
> **cost-data, operations-margin, invoices-and-vendors, count-and-close** — and
> the seven retirements keep their URLs, their files and their external
> backlinks while leaving the taxonomy. **No slug is renamed.**
> `operations-margin` changes its display name to "Food Cost & Margin" and keeps
> its slug, because that hub carries 70 inbound editorial links and slugs are
> final-forever. The proposal is `data/topics.proposed.json`; the migration is
> `scripts/migrate-topics.mjs`, which refuses to write without `--force` while
> the deploy is red.

## Context

`data/topics.json` is the smallest file on the site with the largest reach. Eight
scripts read it, and through them it is stamped into the topic chip on every
glossary term, the `ItemList` on `/learn/topics/`, the eyebrow above every
article, the `DefinedTermSet`, the "See also" rails, and the cluster ids in
`data/tools.json`. It is the first thing a crawler or a language model reads to
decide what this company is about.

It says this company is about local SEO and brand design.

Measured:

- **9 topics. 6 name the retired web-design/SEO line**: `speed-mobile`,
  `conversions`, `local-seo`, `trust-reviews`, `brand-design`, `ai-search`.
- **5 of those 6 hub pages are ALREADY `noindex`** — and every one still holds
  **27–46 inbound editorial links**, because freezing a page did not remove it
  from the taxonomy that *generates the links to it*. `/learn/topics/local-seo/`:
  `indexable: false`, `inboundInternalLinks: 46`.
- 419 pages carry retired-line vocabulary; 3,611 hits, of which **1,688 are the
  local/GBP category alone** (`data/surface-inventory.json#summary.retiredLine`).
- 104 of 171 EN glossary terms are off-thesis; 53 sit squarely in the
  `retired-web-SEO` cluster (`data/content-intent.json`).
- 6 of the 10 glossary sections in `data/library-tags.json` point at a topic that
  is being retired.

Freezing pages was the right move and it was not enough. The pages went quiet;
the *classification* kept talking.

## The decision

### The four that survive

| slug | display | change | EN pages today |
|---|---|---|---:|
| `cost-data` | Cost Data & Sources | kept; blurb extended to name the refusals surface | 189 |
| `operations-margin` | **Food Cost & Margin** | **slug unchanged**, display renamed | 144 |
| `invoices-and-vendors` | Invoices & Vendors | **new** | 34 |
| `count-and-close` | Counting & the Close | **new** | 12 |

`invoices-and-vendors` exists because the vendor mix **is** the buyer definition:
the 919-document corpus contains zero Sysco/US Foods/PFG invoices, and its top
ten are I. Halper, Chefs' Warehouse, Baldor, Pat LaFrieda, Edward Don, Fresco,
Montgomery County DLC, Cintas, Staples, Cozzini Bros. Today those 115 invoice and
vendor pages have no topic of their own and are scattered under `local-seo` and
`operations-margin`.

`count-and-close` is the smallest topic on the site and the only one that grows.
`Beginning + Purchases − Ending = Usage` is what the company sells, and
`/close/`, `/close/specimen/`, `/close/limits/` and `/close/apply/` all land here.
A taxonomy that has no home for the product is not a taxonomy.

### The seven that retire

`speed-mobile`, `conversions`, `local-seo`, `trust-reviews`, `brand-design`
(all five already `noindex`), plus:

- **`ai-search`** — still indexed, and the hardest call. Its subject is earning
  citation in ChatGPT/Perplexity answers. That is a **traffic objective**, and
  traffic is killed as an objective (ADR-025). Keeping a topic whose entire
  purpose is reach, on a site whose doctrine is that credibility does not convert
  into demand, is precisely the contradiction the taxonomy exists to remove.
- **`information-security`** — still indexed. The *subject* survives; the topic
  does not. "Should I type real numbers into a free tool" is a trust question,
  and trust now lives on `/security/`, `/privacy/`, `/methods/` and
  `/close/limits/` — company surfaces dispositioned `keep`, which a reviewer
  actually reads. A CPA checks `/security/`. He does not check a learn hub.

### What retiring means

Each retired topic's `/learn/topics/<slug>/` and `/es/learn/topics/<slug>/` page
**keeps its URL and its file**. It stops being regenerated by
`scripts/build-library.mjs` (which writes only `topicsDoc.topics`), stops
receiving topic chips from articles and glossary terms, and becomes an internal
orphan that still answers any external backlink. Five of the seven are already
`noindex`; `ai-search` and `information-security` are set `noindex` by this
migration, consistent with disposition rule R7.

**Nothing is deleted.** A deletion that burns inbound-link equity for tidiness is
a bad trade — the same rule as ADR-025 §2.

### No slug is renamed

`operations-margin` is the site's most valuable hub (70 inbound editorial links,
parent of the three highest-value pages). Renaming it to `menu-and-margin` would
have been tidier and would have broken every one of those links plus any external
citation. The display name changes; the slug does not. Its old blurb was written
for a website-services company — "the website's role in protecting margin" — and
that clause is what actually needed removing.

## The migration

`scripts/migrate-topics.mjs` runs a **preflight** before it will write anything:

1. no surviving slug is a rename in disguise;
2. every existing topic either survives or appears in `retiredTopics` — no
   silent drops;
3. every `data/tools.json` cluster id still resolves (**measured: there is
   exactly one, `operations-margin`, and it survives** — `build-tools-index.mjs:47`
   throws on a miss);
4. every glossary section in `data/library-tags.json` has a remap decision.

Preflight passes today. It also reports the honest cost: **19 glossary term
overrides lose every topic** and will render without a chip (`wcag-contrast`,
`domain-name`, `open-graph`, `gbp`, `nap-consistency`, `lighthouse`, …). Those
are retired-line terms whose pages are already frozen; losing the chip is the
correct outcome, and naming them beats discovering them.

Nine of the ten glossary sections remap. Two are genuine improvements rather than
removals:

- `restaurant-numbers`: `["operations-margin"]` → `["operations-margin", "count-and-close"]`
  — prime cost and food cost gain the close, which is what those numbers are *for*.
- `data-literacy`: `["brand-design"]` → `["cost-data"]` — reading a chart honestly
  was filed under brand-design, which was always wrong.

Ordering is not optional and is recorded in
`data/topics.proposed.json#_ordering`: remap `library-tags.json` and the tools
cluster label **first**, write the two new pillar essays, **then** swap
`topics.json`, then re-run the eight builders (including `inject-knit-rail.mjs`,
which CLAUDE.md names the highest-churn manual `(idem)` builder), then
`check-all`.

## Alternatives rejected

**Leave the taxonomy and rely on the frozen pages.** Freezing removed the pages
from the index and left the classification generating 184 inbound editorial links
to retired-line hubs. That is the measured status quo, and it is why this ADR
exists.

**Delete the seven hub pages.** Burns 209 inbound editorial links and any
external backlink, to buy nothing a `noindex` and a taxonomy removal do not
already buy.

**Rename `operations-margin`.** Tidier, and it breaks the single most linked hub
on the site. Slugs are final-forever.

**Collapse to two topics (cost-data + the close).** Loses the buyer's own
vocabulary. `invoices-and-vendors` is the page family a prospect with eight to
twelve specialty vendors recognises himself in, and recognition is the whole job
of a qualification surface.

## Consequences

- `data/topics.json` is not yet changed. This ADR is **Proposed**, blocked on
  Q-005 (green deploy). A taxonomy swap landing on a red deploy cannot be
  verified, and `scripts/migrate-topics.mjs` enforces that with a required
  `--force`.
- Two pillar essays must be written in the founder's voice before the swap
  (`data/topic-essays.json`). **This is the only part of the migration an agent
  cannot finish alone.**
- `check-pillar-coverage.mjs:42` will flag every article still tagged to a
  retired pillar until `library-tags.json` is remapped in the same commit.
- The `/learn/topics/` hub drops from nine cards to four. Two new topic pages
  appear; five stop regenerating and are left on disk.
- `data/tools.json`'s single cluster keeps its id and needs its `label_en` /
  `label_es` updated to "Food Cost & Margin" in the same commit, or the tools
  index and the topic page disagree.
