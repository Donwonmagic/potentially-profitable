# ADR-010 — The Cost Index weekly dispatch as a citable, "of-record" publication

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-07-10
- **Owner:** Editorial Lead (Cost-Index publication)
- **Review by:** 2026-10-10
- **Relates to:** ADR-000 (editorial doctrine, fact gate); `docs/cost-index-publication-spec.md` (the full design spec — this ADR is the decisions-of-record digest); `docs/cost-index-value-strategy.md`, `docs/cost-index-methodology.md`, `docs/cost-index-confidence-canon.md`, `docs/cost-index-history-map.md`

> Decision: elevate the machine-generated weekly Restaurant Cost Index dispatch
> (`blog/cost-index-week-<asOf>/`, byline Don Goldstein) from a sequence of
> disconnected snapshots into a continuous, versioned, citable market publication —
> **without ever trading the honesty contract for polish.** Build the longitudinal
> spine, a citable driver layer, a gated human seat, and the citability surfaces; wire
> them into the automation so every future edition inherits them; and gate the two
> highest-risk surfaces (drivers, human note) with dedicated fact gates.

## Context

The dispatch is generated by `scripts/build-cost-index-dispatch.mjs` from the measured
index (`data/cost-index.json`), one dated post per week. Its entire value is **trust
against conflicted incumbents** — it tells an operator a real market move from a vendor
markup, with nothing to sell them. The north star (per the spec) is five-part: a
continuous versioned time series; a transparent stable methodology; calibrated sourced
commentary; tiered depth; stable addressable citability.

The pre-existing dispatch was honest but thin against that bar: the two live editions
(2026-06-05, 2026-06-16) were **deliberately non-commensurable** (different baseline
windows, the basket re-anchored Apr 1 → May 1, the panel grew 13 → 82), so nothing could
be cited as a trend; the hub's substantial citability machinery (methodology page,
`feed.json`, per-ingredient `series.json/csv`, Dataset JSON-LD, provenance) was **not
wired into the dispatch**; and two fact-gate leaks were shipping live (a negative read
rendered as "the increase looks real," spoken in EN+ES audio; a degenerate `$5.51–$5.51`
range printed as false precision).

## The decisions

1. **The honesty contract is inviolable; every improvement is expressed through the
   generator.** Public wholesale levels, never delivered price; a read vs each
   ingredient's own baseline window, not week-over-week unless a real archive backs it;
   public USDA/BLS/FRED only, confidence-gated. Improvements are templated/reproducible
   generator or data changes — never one-off hand edits — so the fact gate governs them.

2. **Longitudinal spine** — append-only `data/cost-index-editions.json`, one frozen
   snapshot per `asOf` (basket, spread, per-ingredient `reads` map, and two version
   stamps: `basketWeightsVersion` + `basket.asOf`). Honest week-over-week is computed
   **only across commensurable editions** — same weights version, and a refreshed anchor;
   on an unchanged anchor or a re-weight the generator **withholds the number and states
   why** (`computeWoW`). Reconstructed seed editions (06-05, 06-16) are basket-level only
   and never anchor a per-ingredient WoW claim. `flag.elevatedWeeks` is surfaced ("N weeks
   running") as the first longitudinal claim that needed no archive.

3. **Two fact-gate leak fixes.** `flagReason()` is sign-aware (a negative re-price read
   never says "the increase looks real"); `dollarPhrase()` suppresses a degenerate range
   and labels it "single-source."

4. **Citable driver layer** — `data/cost-index-drivers.json`, a catalog of **standing,
   public-sourced correlations** (never an invented dated event). The generator attaches a
   driver to a flagged mover **only when** the ingredient is in `affects` AND its measured
   direction agrees with `directionExpected`. This is the Urner-Barry discipline made
   mechanical: the dramatic story is *withheld when the number disagrees* — e.g. eggs
   easing are excluded from the HPAI line even though HPAI is the textbook egg driver.
   Every line reads "association, not a measured cause" with a `<details class="cite">`
   drawer (source + `retrievedAt`, TLD-only gov URLs). **Do-not-assert line:** never a
   magnitude attributed to a driver, never causation, never a forecast.

5. **A gated human seat** — optional `data/cost-index-editors-notes.json` keyed by `asOf`,
   rendered as a "From the floor — Don Goldstein" block, **absent by default**. It
   interprets what is on the page and may not introduce a new number, ingredient, or
   forecast.

6. **Tiered depth + citability wired into the dispatch** — per-edition `Dataset` JSON-LD
   (`isPartOf` the hub series), a "Cite this edition" block (versioned-basket disclosure +
   sources + permalink + reproducibility), a "Go deeper" tier linking the per-ingredient
   pages / feed / data, a citable per-week snapshot (`cost-index/week-<asOf>.json` + `.csv`,
   CC0), and an EN+ES edition archive at `/cost-index/weekly/`
   (`scripts/build-cost-index-archive.mjs`).

7. **Two dedicated fact gates, in `check-all`** (both negative-tested to prove they reject
   violations): `scripts/check-cost-index-drivers.mjs` (catalog schema + freshness +
   "no driver prose without a catalog entry") and `scripts/check-cost-index-editors-note.mjs`
   (numeric traceability to the edition snapshot / `sourced-claims.json`, no-forecast, bio
   discipline).

8. **Automation persists it all.** Both `cost-index-refresh.yml` (daily self-heal) and
   `cost-index-dispatch.yml` (Tuesday publish) run the archive + hero builds, run the two
   new gates before publish, and **widen the scoped commit** to stage the spine, the
   per-week dataset, and the archive — otherwise the generator wrote them and
   `git checkout -- .` threw them away. Confirmed working: the 2026-07-06 edition published
   automatically with the full feature set.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Fully-generated only (no human seat).** Safest for the fact gate, but forfeits the one
  thing a data feed can't supply — a named operator who buys these ingredients. Resolved by
  making the human seat *structured and gated* rather than free prose.
- **Fake week-over-week across a discontinuity.** Rejected outright — a delta across a
  re-anchor or re-weight compares two different rulers. The spine's version stamps exist
  precisely to withhold it.
- **Event-specific driver claims** (e.g. "CBP logged a slowdown on <date>"). Rejected — that
  is a fabrication risk. The catalog carries only *standing* mechanisms with real public
  sources; current directional claims come only from the measured read.
- **Running `sync-includes` in the dispatch workflow.** Rejected — its `_includes` footer
  template drifts vs the live count sentinels and would regress them; the generators copy
  chrome from live donor pages instead.

## Consequences / walk receipt

- **Verification limits (this container):** no browser/deploy walk. Correctness here is code
  reasoning + `node scripts/check-all.mjs` + the two new gates' negative tests + prettier —
  never a live device walk. A pushed branch is not live: `apps/web`-equivalent surfaces
  reach production only after merge **and** the Cloudflare deploy.
- **Preconditions on the founder, not code:** fresh data needs `FRED_KEY`/`BLS_KEY`/`AMS_KEY`
  repo secrets (the daily refresh no-ops without them, holding last-good); the weekly email
  needs `COST_INDEX_BROADCAST_SECRET`.
- **Recurring human upkeep:** driver `retrievedAt` is bumped **manually** on source
  re-review (the gate warns at >365 days). The catalog carries `label_es`/`mechanism_es`
  for future ES parity; the dispatch is **EN-only** today, so those fields are not yet
  rendered or gated.
- **Per-ingredient WoW lights up going forward** now that native editions (06-18, 07-06)
  carry a `reads` map; reconstructed seeds never back it.
- **Live operational status is tracked elsewhere, not frozen here** — see the storefront
  board (`docs/`-recorded board note, 2026-07-09 flagged a frozen data heartbeat + a deploy
  blocker). This ADR records the *architecture and decisions*; the board records *whether
  the heartbeat is beating today.*
- `check-all` carries a pre-existing baseline of glossary/topic/hub schema idempotency
  failures unrelated to this work; the publication work introduced none.

## Result

The dispatch is now a continuous, versioned, reproducible series with a citable dataset per
edition, an archive, a sourced-and-direction-gated driver layer, and a gated operator voice
— all generated, all fact-gated, all inherited automatically by every future weekly edition.
Full design detail and the prioritized roadmap live in `docs/cost-index-publication-spec.md`.
