## TL;DR
- Lowest long-term cost + full ownership + top page-speed, no built-in ordering needed → **custom hand-built** (Muntin): you own the HTML/CSS, hosting ~$0–10/mo, no platform rent; bolt on ordering/reservations from a specialist only if needed.
- Cheap DIY you maintain, low ordering volume → **Squarespace** (best design, 0% transaction fee on commerce tiers) or **Wix** (more restaurant features).
- Priority is direct online ordering, prefer flat fee over commissions → **Owner.com** ($499/mo flat, 0% restaurant commission) or **Toast** if you also want the POS. **BentoBox**/**Popmenu** are strong on restaurant-specific design/marketing but cost more once add-ons stack + lock you in more.

## How to choose (by goal)
- Great-looking fast site that ranks, mostly dine-in/phone → **custom/hand-built**.
- Tiny op, DIY tonight, budget is everything → **Squarespace** or **Square Online**.
- Online ordering is the business, hate commissions → **Owner.com** (high volume) or **Toast** (also want POS).
- Upscale, design/brand everything, pay for hands-on → **BentoBox**.
- Marketing on autopilot → **Popmenu** (read the 12-month contract first).
- Rule: the more a platform bundles (site+ordering+POS+marketing), the more convenient AND the harder/pricier to leave. Custom keeps pieces decoupled.
