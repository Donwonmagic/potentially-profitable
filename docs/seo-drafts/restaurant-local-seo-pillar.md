## TL;DR
Restaurant local SEO is the work that gets your restaurant named when someone nearby searches for a place to eat — in the three-pin Map Pack, the blue links, and now the AI answer above both. Four things move it most: a claimed, complete Google Business Profile with the right primary category; a steady stream of recent reviews you respond to; a real website Google can read (not a PDF); and answers in plain text an AI assistant can quote. Ads and tricks don't move it; the slow, real signals do.

## Key takeaways
- The Google Business Profile does most of the local-search work for an independent restaurant — claim it, complete it, pick the most specific primary category first.
- The primary category is the single biggest Map-Pack lever. "Italian Restaurant" beats "Restaurant" every time.
- Reviews are a ranking factor (count + recency), and Google reads your response rate as a sign of an active business.
- Search Console is the free dashboard most operators never open — the leverage is usually in CTR, not position.
- Presence ≠ inclusion: a 2026 Uberall study found 83% of restaurants never surface in AI search though 86% keep a Google listing.
- None of this is an agency job or an ad budget — a ~15-minute weekly habit plus a few one-time fixes.
