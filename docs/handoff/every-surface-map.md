## 4. CROSS-SURFACE SYSTEMIC ISSUES (fix once)

These recur across nearly every cluster; each is one fix applied globally, and most are P0 trust-debt an honesty brand cannot ship.

**P0 — honesty defects live today (near-zero-risk fixes, do before any redesign):**
1. **Stale "Father's Day weekend 2026" batch banner** — hard-coded in static HTML on the vast majority of surfaces (verified: all 5 marketing pages, 8 of 9 trust pages, `sign-in`, 17 of 18 blog files, and injected into blog bodies). Father's Day was June 21; today is July 10–11. Points at a retired SEO dispatch. The exact "smudge on the glass" retention-strategy §do-first #0 names. **Remove globally.**
2. **Fire-and-forget capture handlers that fabricate success** — the shared newsletter/founding-list handler does `fetch().then(() => state='ok')` with silent `.catch`; `fetch` resolves on 4xx/5xx, so "check your inbox to confirm your spot" fires on a 500. Live on home, about, security, ledger, cost-index hub. **The sign-in/account flows are the gold standard (`res.ok` check + persistent error element) — copy them.** The single most on-brand-damaging live defect.
3. **Cadence contradiction** — stated 4+ ways simultaneously: "Monday/Wednesday/Friday," "weekly read," "four notes a quarter," and the now-canonical **monthly** (first Tuesday). Lives inside single clusters (cost-index hub says monthly, footer says quarterly) and in CLAUDE.md/automation (still weekly). **Reconcile to: publication = monthly; source cadence = each series' native rhythm (explained on methodology); + spike alerts.**
4. **Stale flagship anchor** — the cost-index hub headline opens on a ~10-week-old basket (`2026-05-01`, +0.1%, medium confidence) that contradicts the site's own current July edition (−5.0%, high) and every ingredient page (2026-07-06). Fix structurally: feed the composite from the freshest edition; render a designed "held — here's why" state, never a silent stale number.

**Design system (one Phase-1 re-pigment, not a rebuild):** every surface is warm-editorial — `--teal #2A50C8`, `theme-color #2A50C8`, Fraunces serif + Inter, cream ground. This is the "emotionless" register the founder flagged. Target: slate ramp + electric-blue `#3b68f5` + Inter tabular numerics, dark-first. The auth trio and much of the app already reference the semantic token layer, so re-pigment is accent+type swap. **Exceptions that keep serif by design:** `about/` (memoir) and `methods/` (masthead).

**Trust-defensiveness → ambient (the founder's emotional pivot):** ~90% of the trust cluster and the honesty apparatus on every blog post is the company arguing *about itself* on its back foot. Move reassurance to the point of decision — "your invoices stay yours / trains nothing / no rake" as quiet microcopy on the Ledger upload and tool results, stated once. Collapse nine defense walls into one calm hub + a few genuinely distinct proof panes. Turn rigor into *craft* (inspectable figures), not copy.

**"Same page N times" (the window-with-many-panes mandate):** the two biggest offenders are the trust cluster (9 near-identical gray panes) and the glossary (172 clones). The per-surface registers in §1 are the fix — same tokens + mullions, different jobs.

**Retired-era vocabulary in copy AND structured data:** "studio," "Lead Designer," "site audits / menu copy," "case studies," "book a 20-minute call," "which website platform," `knowsAbout: ["Restaurant website design","SEO",…]` in JSON-LD (about, home, system, ai, methods, changelog, learn, blog hub). **Sweep copy + schema together.**

**Async-model contradictions:** multiple "book a call / 20 minutes" CTAs (learn hub, learn/start-here, system, blog hub, glossary knit) contradict the Window's "reply within 4 hours" async model. Reconcile to the CTA canon.

**Number/count drift (an honesty brand cannot miscount itself):** security counts its own claims 4 ways (nine/ten/10/1–9-then-11); topic counts 6/7/8/9; KPI count 7 vs 4 (no changelog entry for the change); widgets "19/19" vs 18 actual; "six languages" audio (fr/it/pt/zh cut 2026-06-17) still claimed in system, doordash JSON-LD, may-2026-wave, get-found; "14 years / a decade / ~9.5 yrs" operator experience; sheets "31/30/thirty-one." **One reconciliation pass.**

**Structured-data hygiene:** `trust/` WebPage description is the AI page's text (copy-paste contamination); stale AudioObject language claims; missing Person JSON-LD on studio. **Audit heads alongside copy.**

**Uneven JS payload:** methods/receipts/changelog don't load `site.js` (search/Cmd-K, ES banner, mobile menu differ across one cluster). Standardize.

**Self-redirecting internal links:** blog hub + glossary "Read" column emit `/blog/<slug>/` hrefs for ~26–50+ articles that live in `/library/`, relying on a hand-maintained 182-line Worker redirect map. They 301 in production but read as 404s to static crawlers/LLM ingestion/link-checkers, and any unmapped slug is a true 404. ES side is defensive; EN punts. **Teach EN to emit canonical `/library/` hrefs.**

**Dead internal links to pruned tools:** sheets pairs-with rails + course lessons point at ~10 retired tools (brand-suite, gbp-grader, seo-grader, menu-converter, etc.) with no directories. Sweep.

**Retired-funnel taxonomy in admin/ops:** admin/window resolve-outcomes ("Contract/Call"), the lead-to-call KPI, and the field-notes "tribe" social-proof risk all encode the dead lead-gen funnel. Realign to product/retention metrics; gate field-notes so it never fabricates volume.

**Graphics canon:** strong and gate-compliant where it applies (blog, glossary explainers, cost-index) — *not* the problem. The trust cluster is the opposite: near-zero data-viz vocabulary (only security has a table/grid) — world-class substance stranded in gray body text. The fix is adding `viz-*` instruments to trust panes, not fixing violations.
