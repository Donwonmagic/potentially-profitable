# Regulatory, Liability & Professional Standards

**Verdict: MAJOR_REVISION**

## Thesis
The verdict changes what Muntin sells from software to a professional deliverable, and it does that without once using the words legal, counsel, licence, regulation, insurance, liability, indemnity, LLC or tax — I grepped the full verdict JSON and the counts are zero for every one of those. It handles exactly one legal question (employer consent to publish a specimen) and none of the other eight the pivot creates. Two of those are potentially disqualifying as written. First, the framing: Maryland's practice-of-accountancy definition reaches "providing a written certificate or opinion offering positive or negative assurance … on the correctness of the information or on the fairness of the presentation of the information in a financial statement, report, schedule, or an exhibit," with civil penalties up to $5,000 per violation [UNVERIFIED — secondary sources, needs the statutory text confirmed]. The verdict's own words are "a dated, signed statement … sold the way an audit firm sells an opinion," plus a "signed Ed25519 workpaper export." A nonlicensee may prepare financial statements *without issuing a report*, and COMAR 09.24.01.10 appears to give a compilation safe harbour if the report carries "The preparer of this report has not undergone and is not required to undergo peer review" and never references SSARS — so the commercial promise survives, but only if the words change. The framing that makes the strategy sellable is the framing that puts it at the licensing line. Second, and nobody has caught this: the strategy's delivery mechanism is "an agent-run exception desk" in which sessions author vendor templates from the invoices the deterministic engine could not read. That is an LLM reading customer invoice content. `apps/web/lib/copy.ts:3888` publishes "Your invoice content never reaches a language model," `:3082` adds "we have no model to train," 93 storefront pages carry the claim, and `docs/sub-processors.md` states "The deterministic v4 architecture removed the LLM sub-processor (Anthropic) entirely; no third party processes invoice content." The plan discloses the desk on `/close/` and nowhere in the DPA, the sub-processor list, or the ROPA — turning the company's single load-bearing honesty claim into a false one at the exact moment it is sold as the product. Underneath both sits an entity that three documents describe three different ways: `LICENSE` says Muntin Digital LLC, `/home/user/potentially-profitable/terms.html:423` says "a Maryland LLC," `docs/tos.md:135` says Delaware is "Muntin Digital LLC's home state," and `docs/legal-counsel-kickoff.md:359` asks the founder for "confirmation an entity EXISTS (if the product is currently operated as a sole proprietorship, counsel should advise on entity formation FIRST — it gates everything)" — a checklist item that has sat open since roughly 2026-06-09. Signing a number personally, out of an entity whose existence and state are unconfirmed, against a valuation the audit already establishes is biased low three ways, is the precise fact pattern that produces personal liability. The strategy is right that credibility is the closing asset. It has not noticed that a signature is a legal instrument, not a design flourish.

## Neglected
- **[DISQUALIFYING] The deliverable's framing sits on the Maryland attest/report line, and the verdict picked the riskiest available words on purpose**
  - Evidence: The verdict says the product is "a dated, signed statement of food cost … sold the way an audit firm sells an opinion," grafts a "signed Ed25519 workpaper export," and adopts the positioning "Every leg foots, or we withhold the number." Maryland Bus. Occ. & Prof. Title 2 / COMAR 09.24 define practice of certified public accountancy to include "providing a written certificate or opinion offering positive or negative assurance … on the correctness of the information or on the fairness of the presentation of the information in a financial statement, report, schedule, or an exhibit"; the Board may assess civil penalties up to $5,000 per violation. A nonlicensee may perform "the preparation of financial statements without the issuance of reports," and COMAR 09.24.01.10 appears to exempt a compilation whose report says "The preparer of this report has not undergone and is not required to undergo peer review" and does not reference SSARS. [UNVERIFIED — secondary sources; statutory text and the Board's current position must be confirmed.] The verdict contains zero occurrences of licence/regulation/counsel/attorney.
  - Consequence: Inventory valuation and food cost are exactly "a schedule" and "an exhibit." A Board complaint — most plausibly filed by the very CPA the strategy courts as reviewer — would not need to prove harm, only that a nonlicensee issued a signed report offering assurance on the correctness of financial information for a fee. The entire GTM (CPA as gatekeeper) puts the complainant in the room by design.
- **[DISQUALIFYING] The agent-run exception desk makes Anthropic an undisclosed sub-processor and falsifies the company's single load-bearing claim**
  - Evidence: The verdict's delivery model is "an agent-run exception desk … A session may author a vendor template, a pack-parse mapping, a catalog synonym" — which requires the session to read the customer invoice the deterministic engine failed on. Against that: `apps/web/lib/copy.ts:3888` "Your invoice content never reaches a language model"; `:3082` "we have no model to train"; `:4067` "We do not read your invoices with a language model"; 93 storefront HTML files carry the claim; `docs/marketing-claims.md` lists it first under "Verified — say these freely (they are the spine)"; `docs/sub-processors.md` states "The deterministic v4 architecture removed the LLM sub-processor (Anthropic) entirely; no third party processes invoice content." The plan's only disclosure is prose on `/close/`. Sub-processor list, DPA §6, ROPA A2 and the DPIA are untouched.
  - Consequence: DPA §6 requires 30 days' advance notice of a new sub-processor with a right to terminate, and `docs/sub-processors.md` states that once paying customers exist "no founder override" is available — so the desk cannot legally start on day one of the first paying month. Independently, an unqualified present-tense claim contradicted by the delivery mechanism is a textbook FTC §5 deception, and the buyer's own professional adviser is the person most likely to test it. The company would be caught doing the one thing its brand exists to promise it never does.
- **[MAJOR] No entity is confirmed, and the two customer-facing legal documents name different states**
  - Evidence: `LICENSE` (product repo): "Copyright (c) 2026 Muntin Digital LLC." `/home/user/potentially-profitable/terms.html:423`: "Muntin Digital™ is a Maryland LLC … registered in Silver Spring, MD; the LLC's registered agent is on file with the Maryland Department of Assessments & Taxation," and `:475` sets Maryland governing law. `docs/tos.md:135-137`: governed by "the State of Delaware (Muntin Digital LLC's home state)" with disputes in Delaware courts. `docs/legal-counsel-kickoff.md:359` still asks the founder to supply "legal entity name, type, state of formation, registered agent, EIN; confirmation an entity EXISTS."

If no entity exists, or a Delaware LLC is transacting in Maryland without foreign qualification, the liability shield the founder is relying on when he personally signs a number may not be there. An LLC in any case does not shield a member from his own negligent misrepresentation — it shields him from the entity's debts.
- **[MAJOR] Third-party reliance is outside the contract, and no limitation-of-liability clause reaches it**
  - Evidence: The buyer's stated readers are the owner, the bookkeeper, and "the CPA who must not be able to dismantle the number." `docs/tos.md` §6 caps liability at 12 months of fees and is a click-through SaaS agreement between Muntin and the account holder. It caps nothing owed to a lender, an SBA underwriter, a buyer in a sale, or an insurer on a business-interruption claim who relied on a signed inventory figure. A signed statement is precisely the artifact that creates the limited class of intended users §552 reaches, and Muntin would be signing one it knows is biased low.

## Would change my verdict
One written answer, from a Maryland attorney or from the Maryland Board of Public Accountancy itself, to a single question: "Is a dated, monthly statement of inventory valuation and food cost, prepared for a fee by a non-CPA from a client's own invoices and physical count, delivered with a preparer's signature and the sentence 'the preparer has not undergone and is not required to undergo peer review,' the issuance of a report under Bus. Occ. & Prof. Title 2 — and does COMAR 09.24.01.10's compilation safe harbour reach it?" If the answer is that the safe-harbour wording clears it, my verdict moves to ENDORSE_WITH_CONDITIONS and the remaining items are ordinary business hygiene. If the answer is that a signed statement of inventory valuation is a report regardless of the disclaimer, the strategy's central artefact cannot be sold at all in its current form and the seat's verdict becomes REJECT-as-framed. Obtaining it is cheap two ways: a one-hour flat-fee consult with a Maryland business attorney (roughly $250-500, unverified), or a written request for guidance to the Board of Public Accountancy at the Maryland Department of Labor, which costs nothing but a letter and produces the more authoritative answer. Ask it with the exact deliverable wording attached — the answer turns entirely on the words, which is why the wording must be settled before the question is asked.
