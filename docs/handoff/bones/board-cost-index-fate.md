# The Fate of the Cost Index and the Free Data Line

**Verdict: MAJOR_REVISION**

## Thesis
The Cost Index has exactly one load-bearing job under "Muntin sells a closed month," and the verdict never names it: it is the published basis of the market-prior rung in the valuation ladder — the input that lets a close value a no-invoice item, which is the whole commercial argument that the count gets shrunk rather than performed. Everything else the verdict gestures at is either false or unearned. It is not a moat: 100% of the 2,020 live-captured observations come from a single agency (USDA), `data/cost-index-health.json#summary` reports `highEligible: 0` against 20 medium / 62 low / 18 directional, the bulk export is CC0 and the methodology, weighting and shippable bar are all published — one HTTP GET reproduces it, and no ADR in either repo asks whether a competitor with distribution could. It is not a compounding citation asset either: `data/cost-index-editions.json` holds four frozen editions, the last `asOf 2026-07-06`, 32 days behind data at 2026-08-05, and it is appended only by a `workflow_dispatch`-only job that defaults to dry-run — the raw JSON compounds, the dated publication chain does not. The scale question the orchestrator flagged bites hardest here: a 40-customer business needs a 24-slug honest reference and nothing more, while a "toe to toe with the largest" business would need exactly the citation-capture program the verdict killed, and the founder cannot fund both — the verdict's ambiguity defaults the choice to "maintain 100 badly," which is the one option that serves neither branch. The maintenance arithmetic is decisive: directly attributable lines in the audit's own obligation ledger (dispatch 10, picker red 1, frozen-series triage 4, ADR-013 datasets 2, ingredient-state-record 0.5, cross-repo re-vendor 1) total 18.5 h/month before any share of gate upkeep, deploy-red clearing or the 142 `es/cost-index` pages — against a measured capacity of 13-26 h/month of which ~10% of maintenance was actually paid in the last thirty days. The Index alone is 42-47% of the company's total obligation and carries zero revenue mechanism. Worse, it is currently the thing that stops the company shipping: `cost-index-refresh.yml:99` rebuilds `data/cost-index-picker.js`, the `git add` allowlist at :302-311 omits it, `:313 git checkout -- .` discards it, and `node scripts/check-cost-index-picker.mjs` fails at HEAD right now — so the Index reds the storefront deploy every Mon/Wed/Fri, and Phase 0's "clear the red deploy" is written as a one-time act against a recurring cause. The honesty debt is also worse than the verdict recorded and concentrated in places it did not look: `basket.asOf` is 2026-06-01 with `coverage: 1` and `confidence: "medium"` while `_lastReviewed` reads 2026-08-05, 17.8% of basket weight sits on frozen contributors, and `/cost-index/shrimp/` publicly refuses to publish a shrimp price while the basket quietly uses shrimp's 2026-06-01 trend as a contributor. My position: keep the Index, shrink it to the twenty-four slugs the product actually consumes, retire the rest through ADR-021's machinery that has never once fired, kill the hand-written monthly dispatch, and move the CPA conversation to week one — because that single conversation, not any amount of correction work, decides whether this asset has a job at all.

## Would change my verdict
The single fact that would most change my verdict is the CPA answer: does a working restaurant CPA accept a public wholesale market index as a disclosed management estimate inside a signed monthly close? If yes, the Index is genuinely load-bearing, the 24-slug core deserves real investment rather than triage, and my "shrink and freeze the rest" recommendation becomes "shrink, then deepen the 24 toward a second independent agency" — because `data/cost-index-health.json` says 25 of the 100 toHigh blockers read \"add a 2nd independent-agency wholesale $ level on the same basis,\" which is a solvable, bounded data project. If no, the market prior leaves the valuation ladder, the Index has no product function at all, and the correct move is to freeze it as a static dated archive with its corrections ledger intact and stop spending on it entirely. Cost to obtain: two 45-minute calls this week, no artifact required beyond a one-page description of the valuation ladder from ADR-009 and the honest confidence split I measured (13 medium, 11 low, 0 high, all within 14 days). The founder can book both before Phase 0 spends an hour, and the verdict currently schedules this in week seven.
