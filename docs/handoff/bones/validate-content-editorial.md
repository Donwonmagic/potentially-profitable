# Content & Editorial Operations

**READY_WITH_GAPS**

## Thesis
This is the strongest editorial-operations plan this company has produced: `data/surface-disposition.json` gives all 1,327 pages a disposition with a stated rule, the corrections ledger is real and passes (`node scripts/check-corrections-ledger.mjs` → 4 corrections, every one naming a gate), and the honesty debt is 14 items whose status is re-derived from exit codes rather than asserted. The 72-falsehood correction and the audio kill are both correctly scoped. The ongoing editorial obligation after Q-013's suspension is genuinely small on paper — 406 maintained pages of which 306 are template-generated Cost Index, 33 carrying a live fact-gate obligation — and that does fit 13-26 h/month. The 492 library articles and 344 glossary pages are handled honestly: 15 library pages stay keep, 288 freeze indexed, 189 freeze-noindex, and the entire glossary freezes. But the plan has confused releasing the maintenance with un-publishing the claim. 469 frozen pages remain 100% indexable (469 of 469 measured), carrying 286,754 words, 247 dead product CTAs and no fact gate, with no reader-visible marker distinguishing them from the maintained audit file — on a site whose pitch is that it labels what it cannot stand behind. The single most important thing still missing is sharper than that: the plan's own honesty-debt ledger does not contain the class of falsehood the storefront is publishing right now. `node scripts/inject-about-cost-read.mjs --check` prints "would update 2 file(s)" — `about/index.html:875` publishes a Cost Index read stamped `as of 2026-07-21` against data whose newest egg print is 2026-07-27 — while CP-30's question is literally "is the storefront no longer publishing a falsehood?" and its `mustBeTrue` checks only Q-003, Q-007 and Q-008. The checkpoint can pass green over a live wrong number on the audit file.
