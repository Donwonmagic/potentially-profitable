# Execution Feasibility — does the plan survive the calendar?

**Verdict: MAJOR_REVISION**

## Thesis
The strategy is executable; the plan built to execute it is not yet, and the reason is not capacity — it is that the plan's own spine is uncommitted and its verify contract already fails on item one. As of this session, `data/queue.json`, `docs/handoff/QUEUE.md` and `scripts/check-queue.mjs` are all untracked (`git status --porcelain` shows `??` on each), `docs/editorial/decisions/ADR-022-the-queue.md` — cited in QUEUE.md's header as the strategy of record — does not exist on disk at all, and `.claude/hooks/` is an empty directory in both repos even though `check-gate-coverage.mjs:96` asserts in the present tense that check-queue "is invoked instead by .claude/hooks/session-start.sh in BOTH repos." That is line 852 reproduced inside the registry whose entire job is to prevent line 852, on the day the doctrine "pay the honesty debt in full" was adopted. Second, the queue's central promise — "closed only by a verify command that exited 0" — is already broken on Q-007: `check-src-sentinel.mjs` exits 0 today reporting `UNANCHORED=138 DRIFT=21 (ratchet holding)`, while Q-007's own doneWhen demands "zero unanchored annotations"; `--done` runs verify, not doneWhen, so the highest-priority honesty item can be certified closed with 138 unverifiable citations and one sign-flipped published market read still live. Third, the gate rewards the wrong act: `unclaimedHigh()` filters to ready-and-unblocked, so claiming ten items in one command greens it for seven days with zero work done, and *closing* Q-001 unblocks Q-020/21/22/24 and immediately re-reds it — doing the single most valuable thing in the plan makes the only visible health signal worse. Fourth, two of the four Phase-0 founder items are misdiagnosed: all five builders Q-005 names pass `--check` here today (exit 0 each), and Q-006's premise that the keys are missing is falsified by `muntin-cost-index-bot` committing measured reads to main on 2026-08-03 and 2026-08-05 through a workflow every step of which is guarded by `if: steps.keys.outputs.configured == 'true'` — while Q-006's verify demands a ≤2-day-old commit to `data/cost-index-editions.json`, a file appended once per monthly edition that last moved 2026-07-06, so the item cannot be closed by doing it. Fifth, nothing in the queue deploys the product: `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` is four `echo "TODO(B-priv-6)"` stubs behind a two-person approval gate at a one-person company, and its web step still names Vercel while CLAUDE.md says OpenNext/Cloudflare — so the twenty agent-hours of Q-020/21/22/24 reach zero customers until an unqueued, undelegatable founder runs `deploy-api.sh` and `deploy:cf` from his Mac, and Q-030's specimen is then built from a close no buyer can obtain. Sixth, the calendar contradicts the pricing doctrine: "$6,000 billed annually, hand-invoiced, in the May-August seasonal peak" cannot be honored by a Phase 3 that runs weeks 10-13 from 2026-08-07, i.e. 16 October to 6 November — the same off-peak, into-the-Jan-February-trough placement the verdict killed the 2026-11-13 GA for, and the next peak is May 2027. On raw hours the plan-only load (~15h queue + ~3h product deploys + ~5h PR review + ~5h August dispatch) is roughly 28h over eight weeks against 26-52 available and does fit; it fits only because it silently assumes the ~53h/month standing maintenance calendar is suspended, which no queue item does — with maintenance the eight-week demand is ~130h against 26-52, a 2.5-5× deficit. Two hard dates nobody scheduled land inside those eight weeks: `check-cost-index-dispatch-fresh.mjs` passes at 27 of 38 days today and reds around 16 August, demanding a hand-written monthly edition in week 2; and the vendored `cost-index-snapshot.json` (`_asOfRange: 2026-07-18..2026-07-28`, `STALE_AFTER_DAYS = 30`) puts 2 of 24 slugs at the 20-day warn line now and the last 18 over the cliff on 27 August, so a Q-001 walk that slips past week 3 measures a silently degraded valuation ladder and could abort the pilot on an artifact of a stale file.
