# Security Posture

**NOT_READY**

## Thesis
The plan is honest about correctness and capacity and effectively silent about security: zero of the 29 queue items touch a security control, and `grep` over `data/queue.json` returns no hit for reaper, posture, attestation, breach, rotation, escrow, insurance, untrusted, quarantine or blast radius. That silence would be defensible if the service model had not changed — but it has. Selling a hand-delivered signed close means a customer's month of vendor invoices now arrives by some channel the plan never names, is worked by an exception desk staffed by an agent workforce, and produces a statement at a `statementUrl` whose verify command (Q-042) checks only that the field is non-empty. That collides head-on with the company's single load-bearing published promise — `ledger/index.html:612` "No language model ever reads your documents; a CI gate blocks anyone from adding one" — because that gate, `scripts/no-llm-ci.sh`, is a source-import scan over `git ls-files` and cannot see an agent session reading the seven real invoice fixtures Q-002 admits are already committed. Second, the obligations Muntin has already written down are now unowned: `docs/dpa.md:225-235` binds a 72-hour breach notice and a 24-hour audit-chain notice, while `runbooks/incident-response.md:22-25` conditions the pager and the contractor backup on "paid GA" and "first day of paid beta" — the exact milestone ADR-030 withdrew with no replacement date, even as Q-042 puts three paying locations under contract in M3. Third, the plan's own preparatory analysis produced two concrete security designs (`bones/os-agent-safety.md:45-46` untrusted-input quarantine, `:51-52` spend and blast-radius caps) and the queue dropped both, while Q-051 stands up precisely the Issue-fed consumer that §45 said must not run first. Fourth, `docs/security/posture-scorecard.md` scores operational-promise enforcement 1/3 with the reaper's prod state unknown and `/v1/posture` calling itself "a genuine theater surface," and no item moves it. The single most important thing missing is a decision of record on who — or what — is permitted to look at a customer's invoice, published in `sub-processors.md` and gated, because everything else in this domain is downstream of that answer.
