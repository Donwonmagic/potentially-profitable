## Thesis
The plan is the best-instrumented thing this company has produced on correctness and honesty, and it is close to empty on my domain. It defines the buyer precisely (1-3 locations, 8-12 specialty vendors, DMV-clustered — Q-041) and the proof point precisely (three signed, one close delivered, one invoice sent — Q-042), and between those two it defines no mechanism at all: the words prospect, referral, introduction, outbound and network appear zero times across the 46 items of data/queue.json, and the warm network already on file (data/sourced-claims.json:634-637 — The Irish Inn at Glen Echo, Nobu DC, Kapnos Kouzina, DMV independents matching the stated profile) is referenced zero times. The board called this DISQUALIFYING (docs/handoff/bones/board-completeness.md:12) and the plan carried the finding forward without adopting a fix. Q-041 budgets 6 founder-hours, which is 8 x 45 minutes by its own arithmetic, so sourcing, pre-qualification, scheduling, no-shows and follow-up are budgeted at zero. The consequence is worse than a missed number: CP-90's kill criterion infers "the price or the buyer is wrong" from eight conversations with zero signings, and if the real cause is an empty pipeline the founder must record a verdict on evidence that never existed — checkpointPolicy.results offers proceed/amend/kill and no "the pipeline was empty" outcome. On retention the plan simply stops: the last customer-facing item is the first invoice, churn and cancel appear zero times, and the second close, count compliance, the delivery slot, the support channel and restatement have no item. The only document in the company named retention (docs/handoff/retention-strategy.md, classified `reference` in docs/contracts/working-set.json) is entirely about return VISITORS under a traffic model ADR-032 released as an objective. The most important thing still missing is a named, dated source of applicants — without it Phases 0-2 execute perfectly and day 90 is an empty room the plan will misread as a falsified price.
