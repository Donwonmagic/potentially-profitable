# Monetization & Path to First Dollar

**NOT_READY**

## Thesis
The plan is the first artifact in this company's history that traces a literal path to a signature: `data/queue.json` holds an unbroken founder-owned chain — Q-002 → Q-001 → Q-020 → Q-017 → Q-030 → Q-031 → Q-041 → Q-042 — costing 22.5 founder-hours on the longest chain and 24.75 across the whole revenue subgraph, with three dated checkpoints whose kill criteria can end the strategy rather than adjust it. That is real, and the pricing decision (ADR-030) is coherent: one price, monthly, hand-invoiced, no billing code, because forty invoices a month is a spreadsheet and an email. But the path does not reach a dollar. Q-042's own verify command asserts `sentOn` and `amountUsd` and nothing else — the strings "paidOn", "received", "net-30", "net-45", "collect" and "bank" appear nowhere in `data/queue.json`, so CP-90's question "Did anyone pay for a closed month" is satisfied by a PDF emailed to someone who never pays. Three preconditions of invoicing a business for a signed professional deliverable have no queue item at all: the words counsel, entity, LLC, engagement letter, insurance and W-9 return zero hits across all 46 items, while the live `terms.html:423` still describes a retired web-design studio and `terms.html:430` sends the paid product's terms to `ledger.muntin.digital/legal/terms`, a hostname neither repo routes. There is still no named source for the eight conversations Q-041 requires — `board-completeness.md:12` called that DISQUALIFYING and its remedy (name the first ten from Don's DMV history, with an employer-conflict screen) was not adopted; Q-002 obtains consent to process invoice DATA, which is a different question from whether his employment permits selling a competing service to DMV independents. And the day-90 deliverable is arithmetically unreachable: `board-service-delivery.md` measures time-to-first-correct-close at ~60-75 days from `inventory.ts:295-330` and `template_store.py:100`, against customers signed inside M3 (2026-10-07 to 2026-11-06). The single most important thing missing is the instrument and the channel — a document to sell under and a list of people to sell to — because every hour of correctness work in M1 and M2 lands on a path that currently terminates in an unenforceable invoice sent to nobody in particular.
