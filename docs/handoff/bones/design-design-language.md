# The design language itself — the token spine, type system, color construction, pane grammar and signature element shared by muntin.digital (1,327 pages) and Muntin Ledger (apps/web + packages/ui)

## Thesis
A company whose product is a number it will refuse to print should look like a measuring instrument, not like software. An instrument has three properties software does not: a frame that does not move when the reading changes, a scale legible before you know the value, and a held position when there is no reading. Muntin is named for the bar that holds many small panes in one window — which means the company already owns the exact visual metaphor for many small line items resolving into one view, and has spent it on a logo and two pages. The design language should be that metaphor made structural: a PANE is the atom of every surface in both repos, it has exactly three states — glazed (measured), frosted (estimated), unglazed (withheld) — and nothing in the system is ever permitted to remove a pane. A withheld number keeps its slot, its tabular width, its baseline, and gets a STRONGER frame, not a dash. That single rule answers color, empty states, the logo, the closed month and the dark theme at once, and it is the only visual idea available to this company that no competitor can copy, because it comes from the name. The aesthetic risk I am taking is typographic: retire Fraunces from the display slot and move the brand's voice out of the headline and into the apparatus — the eyebrows, column heads, dates, legends, tickmarks and the lockup get a controlled monospace, and the operator's own numbers get the biggest type on the page. The site stops declaiming and starts instrumenting, which is the emotional pivot rendered in type rather than argued in copy. I am also reversing a prior decision of record: `docs/handoff/founder-vision.md:104` calls for unifying on the app's electric blue #3b68f5; #3b68f5 is the median SaaS accent the 2026 slop literature names, #2A50C8 is a deeper stamp-blue that is already the product's own `--mun-accent-text` (tokens.css:76) and already occurs 2,579 times across 376 files — so the less fashionable choice is also the cheaper one. Finally, the reason this is buildable at 13-26 h/month is mechanical, not aesthetic: 88% of the site's color literals are written by generator scripts into per-page style blocks, and three scripts are the named author of most of the drift. You do not repaint 1,327 pages. You edit three files and press run.

## Proposal
**THE INSTRUMENT — one grammar, three states, two registers.**

**1. THE PANE is the atom.** A pane is a rectangle bounded by hairline muntins, `border-radius: 0`, with exactly three states and never a fourth. It is one CSS component, `.pane[data-state]`, shipped in `assets/site.css` and `packages/ui`, consumed at every scale in both repos.

- `data-state="glazed"` — MEASURED. Ground `--surface`. Value in `--ink`, `font-variant-numeric: lining-nums tabular-nums`, right-aligned, decimal-aligned. A tickmark in the mark-face sits top-right: `∑` footed, `✓` traced to invoice. No colour. **A measured number is the only thing on a Muntin surface that gets no decoration at all** — this is the inversion that makes the rest legible.
- `data-state="frosted"` — ESTIMATED. Identical slot, width, baseline, weight and ink. The ground carries a 45° hairline hatch at 6% opacity (`repeating-linear-gradient`, four lines of CSS, no image, no filter). The mark-face label reads `e` and resolves to a legend below the block. **Never a different text colour** — an estimate is not a warning, and colouring it as one is the single most common lie in financial UI.
- `data-state="unglazed"` — WITHHELD. **The pane keeps its exact geometry.** No dash, no spinner, no "—". Ground `--surface-inset`; the surrounding muntin goes from `--line` to `--line-dark` — *the frame around a missing number gets stronger.* The pane contains a three-part line in the mark-face at 11px: what is missing, why it blocks the number, and one repair link. ("No count for this station · usage needs an ending count · Enter it →".) This is the whole design answer to "how does withholding feel like authority," and it is one component, not a redesign.

Withheld is expressed as TEXTURE AND FRAME WEIGHT, never as hue — because colour implies severity and withholding is a decision, not a fault. That constraint is what keeps the palette down to three semantics.

**2. THE SASH is the closed month, and it is the logo at 1:1.** The four legs — Beginning / + Purchases / − Ending / = Usage — drawn as four lights in one sash, right-aligned tabular figures, the transom rule sitting above the derived line at the mark's own 38.3% proportion (`site.css:7975` already computes it), a legend block beneath carrying `∑ ✓ e` and the coverage line. The brand mark and the financial statement become the same drawing. This replaces the three-tile summary in `apps/web/app/(product)/insights/food-cost/food-cost-client.tsx` and draws the two legs the API already returns and the render layer currently discards.

**3. THE COVERAGE BAR is the hero, the OG card and the masthead.** A horizontal strip of N panes, N = line count, glazed / frosted / unglazed in true proportion, with the frontier metric printed beneath in the mark-face: "Answered 87% of lines. Held 13%." One `<div>`, a grid, three classes. It renders the risk-coverage curve as the literal picture of a window. Scale-invariant by construction: 4 panes at 16px is a status chip; 40 panes at 400px is the coverage bar; the sash at 1200px is the close. **One grammar renders the favicon and the audit schedule.** That retires `.window`, `.sash`, and `.mun-pane-*` into a single implementation.

**4. COLOR — OKLCH, one accent, three semantics.**
- Neutral: a 13-step ink ramp constructed in OKLCH at hue ~250°, chroma 0.006–0.012 — cool, near-achromatic, engineered. The current hexes already sit roughly on that line, so light mode barely moves; what changes is that the steps become arithmetic instead of hand-picks, and the DARK ramp is re-derived at matched hue, which kills the warm #F1EDE5 text residue (1,542 occurrences) by construction.
- Accent: **#2A50C8 only.** `--mun-accent:#3b68f5` retires. Five steps derived in OKLCH (soft / border / base / hover / press) replacing the hand-picked `#1F3A93` / `#EAF0FE` / `#eaf0fe` / `#b9ccfa`. Reversing `founder-vision.md:104` on the record: #3b68f5 is the median SaaS blue the slop literature names; #2A50C8 is a stamp, is already `--mun-accent-text`, and is already in 376 files.
- Semantics, rationed to three: `--measured` (= ink; no colour), `--estimated` (one ochre, replacing SIX warn tokens — `--gold`, `--status-warn`, `--status-warn-deep`, `--status-warn-fill`, `--status-warn-tint`, `--gold-dark`), `--exception` (#C42E2E). Withheld gets no hue. If a fourth colour is wanted, the categorisation is wrong.
- Every token declared as `oklch()` with a hex fallback on the preceding line, in ONE file per repo, and `check-tokens-sync.mjs` extended to fail any hex in a colour position outside those two files.

**5. TYPE — three faces, three jobs, and the brand voice moves into the apparatus.**
- `--font-text` → **Inter** (variable, already on disk, 48KB). Body, UI, headings. It is safe precisely because it is no longer the display face.
- `--font-mark` → **one self-hosted variable monospace**, latin subset, ≤40KB, single weight axis. This is NEW and it is the identity. It sets: eyebrows, section tags, index column heads, all dates, figure labels, tickmarks, legends, coverage lines, pane-state reasons, and the lockup. Default recommendation **IBM Plex Mono** (SIL OFL, humanist, drawn to read as engineering rather than terminal); **Martian Mono** if the founder wants a wider display axis and more signature. *I cannot verify licence, metrics or file size from this container — that must be checked before it ships.* This is the `.lib-idx` treatment (`site.css:458-483`) promoted from one strip to the brand's voice.
- `--font-editorial` → **Fraunces**, surviving for exactly two jobs: the italic-on-commit motif (`tokens.css:106`) and the PDF operator sheets. **Fraunces retires as `--font-display`** — 6,293 declarations re-point through one token, which is why the most radical-sounding move here is also the cheapest.
- Scale: **9 steps, no half-pixels** — 11 / 12 / 13 / 15 / 17 / 20 / 26 / 34 / 46, rem-based, two clamp() display steps. Replaces 240 distinct values and 17,300 half-pixel declarations.
- Weight band: **400 / 500 / 560 only.** 600 is reserved for the mark-face at ≤13px. **700 and 800 retire — 7,686 declarations.** Hierarchy moves to size and colour.
- Numerals: `font-variant-numeric: lining-nums tabular-nums` on every numeric cell in both repos (currently 0 `lining-nums`), right-aligned, decimal-aligned. Gated.

**6. STRUCTURE.** Spacing = the product's 4px scale verbatim, adopted as `--sp-*` on the storefront. Radii: 6/6/8 kept; **panes are 0** — only the outer sash rounds, at 6px, matching the mark's corners. Borders: `--rule` (1px `--line`), `--rule-strong` (1px `--line-dark`), `--muntin-weight`. Elevation: hairline-first plus exactly ONE popover shadow; `--elev-1/2/3` AND `--mun-depth-1/2/3` both retire (two parallel elevation systems). Grid: adopt `@container` for the pane and the sash so one component works in the article body, the sidebar and the cost-index template without three variants — this is a maintenance reduction, not a feature.

**7. DARK AND LIGHT AS TWO DESIGNED STATES, TWO DELIBERATE DEFAULTS.** Same tokens, same `data-theme` contract both repos already implement. **Storefront defaults light** (a CPA reads it at a desk, and dark-by-default is on the named AI-tell list); **product defaults dark** (a line at 11pm). Dark is a re-derivation, not an inversion: base at L≈10%, elevation by +3–4 L points and never by shadow, accent lifted in L and desaturated ~25% as an OKLCH derivation of #2A50C8 rather than the current hand-picked #5b82ff.

**8. MOTION.** Keep exactly one signature: the staggered glaze (`mun-pane-in`, `tokens.css:445-496`), which already collapses to 0ms under reduced-motion by construction. Everything else is 180–240ms, transform/opacity, `--ease-out`. `.window`'s five keyframes and twelve nth-child delays retire. No scroll-driven animation, no view transitions until caniuse is checked against a primary support table.

**9. THE FALSIFIER IS A DESIGN ELEMENT.** `/verify/` is one page, set in the mark-face, carrying `no-llm-ci.sh` as a copy-pasteable command a stranger can run in thirty seconds. Every trust claim on the site either carries its falsifier inline or is deleted. This is the visual arm of the honesty contract and it costs one page.

## Risk
**The honest failure mode: this design is colder, and the founder's own diagnosis was that the site reads emotionless.** I am not adding warmth — I am relocating it, out of the serif and the golden-hour wash and into the copy and the operator's own numbers being the biggest thing on the page. If the editorial work does not land alongside the tokens, this ships as a more disciplined, better-gated version of exactly the problem the founder named, and it will be harder to argue with because it will look deliberate. The pane grammar is the mitigation — an unglazed pane with a repair path is an emotional object, it says *we looked and we are not going to guess at you* — but only if the copy inside it is written by a person who has been on a floor at month-end. Tokens cannot carry that.

Second risk: **one idea applied everywhere is a monotony risk.** The pane grammar earns its place where there is something to withhold. On the 492 library articles and 344 glossary terms — which are prose, not readings — it has nothing to say, and applied there it becomes decoration, which is the failure mode I am claiming to design against. The discipline has to be that the pane appears only where a number could have been and was not.

Third risk, and the one most likely to actually bite: **the mono mark-face is unverified.** I could not fetch a foundry, a licence, a metrics table or a real file size from this container. IBM Plex Mono is a recommendation from memory, not a measurement. If it lands wrong — too wide, too techy, bad at 11px, a 60KB latin subset — the whole typographic thesis needs a different face, and the fallback is Inter with tracking and small-caps in the mark slot, which is duller but safe. Check the file before writing the `@font-face`.

Fourth: **I am reversing a founder decision of record.** `founder-vision.md:104` chose electric blue. My argument is that #3b68f5 became the median between when that was written and now, and that #2A50C8 costs 2,579 fewer edits. That argument is about fashion drift, which is exactly the kind of reasoning that produces the next reversal. If the founder disagrees, the accent is one token and the rest of this design is unaffected — nothing else here depends on which blue wins.

Fifth, and structural: **the full sequence is ~19 founder-hours against a 16h month-one window that already carries 2.5h of unpriced overhead.** It does not fit one month, and it must DISPLACE queue items rather than be added to them. If it is added, the honest prediction is the historical one: 26% close, and the site ends up carrying both the old system and half the new one, which is measurably worse than either.
