# Product Truth — claimed vs shipped

**NOT_READY**

## Thesis
The plan is genuinely strong on one half of product truth and structurally blind to the other. Storefront DATA honesty is instrumented better than most companies ever manage: `docs/handoff/honesty-debt/honesty-debt.json` carries 14 items with status re-derived from exit codes, Q-003/Q-007/Q-008 correct real published falsehoods, and Q-025 (/close/limits/) invents a genuinely novel artifact. But every one of those 14 debts is a Cost Index or storefront-publishing debt. The company's PRODUCT claims — the ones in `apps/web/lib/copy.ts` and on 91 storefront pages — have no equivalent ledger, and `docs/marketing-claims.md`, the registry whose entire job is claimed-vs-shipped, appears zero times in `data/queue.json`. Worse, the plan's own delivery mechanism creates the largest new false claim in company history: an agent-run exception desk means a language model reads customer invoice content, falsifying `copy.ts:3082/3888/4067`, 91 storefront pages, row 1 of the "Verified — say these freely" registry, and `docs/sub-processors.md:43` — while `no-llm-ci.sh` stays green and certifies it. The board's regulatory seat named this and wrote "Do not ship the verdict's version, which is (i) with only (ii)'s disclosures" (`phase-f-board.json:71`); the queue encodes exactly that version, with the words "exception desk" appearing in `data/queue.json` precisely four times, all inside one field of Q-072's rationale. Second: Q-072 (/close/, the page that publishes "every leg foots or we withhold") is blocked on Q-001/Q-005/Q-010 but NOT on Q-020/Q-021/Q-022/Q-024, the four defects that make the promise untrue — while Q-030 (the specimen) IS blocked on all four. The single most important thing still missing is a decision, in a ratified ADR, on whether the exception desk reads raw invoice content or values-stripped layout, made BEFORE any page sells the close.
