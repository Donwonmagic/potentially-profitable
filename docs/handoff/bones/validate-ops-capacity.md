# Operations & Founder Capacity

**READY_WITH_GAPS**

## Thesis
On the demand side this is the most disciplined capacity artifact the company has ever produced: every item in `data/queue.json` carries a declared `founderHours` and a `window`, `policy.founderHoursAreDeclared` forbids parsing hours out of prose, `capacity.floorPlan` writes the drop order in advance so a cut is made by decision rather than drift, and Q-013 is the item nothing else in fifteen months did — an explicit written suspension of the ~53h/month standing calendar that every prior plan silently assumed. I verified the sums: M1 = 14.5h declared + 2.5h overhead = 17.0h, M2 = 11.25h, M3 = 11.0h, ninety-day total 39.25h. Against the declared 13h floor that is 100.6% of available capacity for the quarter and 131% of the floor in M1 — the plan does not fit the floor it says it is sized against, and the floorPlan's own 4h of savings is half re-estimate (Q-001 "lands at its 4h lower bound" for the one task nobody has performed in fifteen months). The supply side is asserted, not measured: 13-26h/month traces to a single prose line dated 2026-07-05, `capacity.realizedRate` records that ~3 of 31 maintenance-hours-due were paid in the 30 days to 2026-08-07, and that 10% figure is used only to justify Q-013, never to discount the budget. Every one of the six kill criteria tests the product, the reviewer, or the buyer; not one tests whether the founder delivered the hours — the binding constraint is the only thing in the plan with no falsifier. Two kill criteria are worse than absent: `deskMinutesPerClose > 60` at CP-60 and `> 90` at CP-90 are both `decidedBy: measurement` against a number nothing in either repo produces, no queue item instruments, and whose verify script (`scripts/check-specimen-close.mjs`) does not exist. The single largest structural omission is day 91: Q-013's suspension expires 2026-11-06, the plan ends 2026-11-06, and `ADR-033` contains zero occurrences of resume, recurring, steady, or M4 — so the moment three locations sign, the standing calendar returns *and* a recurring close obligation begins, against unchanged capacity, with no target for what standing obligation should be by then. Bus factor is not addressed at all: succession, continuity, insurance and CODEOWNERS appear zero times in `data/queue.json`, and CP-90's success condition puts three paying restaurants behind a KMS CMK the runbooks say is unrecoverable if lost, against a drill never run.

## Gaps
- **[BLOCKING] The plan consumes 100.6% of the founder-hour floor it claims to be sized against, and the denominator itself is one unmeasured prose line.**
  - data/queue.json#capacity totals verified by summing `founderHours` + `reviewOverheadPerMonth`: M1 17.0h, M2 11.25h, M3 11.0h = 39.25h against 3 × 13h floor = 39h. `capacity.founderHoursPerMonth.source` is 'docs/seo-handoff-both-repos.md:23 (2026-07-05) — ~a few hours/week' — a prose estimate, not a measurement. `capacity.realizedRate` = 0.10 (audit line 92: ~3 of 31 maintenance-hours-due paid in 30 days) is recorded and then used only to justify Q-013, never to discount any window.
  - Cost: Zero slack for illness, a Tacombi schedule change, an item overrun, or the unpriced onboarding of the first customers. The first overrun in M1 does not trigger a decision; it silently pushes into M2, which is how prior audits closed at 26%.
- **[BLOCKING] Day 91 is an unpriced cliff: the maintenance suspension expires the same day the plan ends, and a recurring close obligation begins.**
  - Q-013 doneWhen requires `from:` and `until:` covering M1-M3, i.e. expiring 2026-11-06 — the exact `capacity.windows.M3.to`. `docs/editorial/decisions/ADR-033-the-ninety-days-and-its-falsifiers.md` (243 lines) contains zero occurrences of resume, recurring, steady, M4, or day 91. Q-042 signs three locations at $600/month inside M3, creating a monthly close obligation that no window budgets.
  - Cost: On 2026-11-07 the founder faces the resumed ~53h/month calendar plus three recurring closes plus support plus onboarding, against 13-26h — a worse position than the 2.5-5x deficit the plan was built to escape, now with paying customers who can be let down in public.
- **[BLOCKING] deskMinutesPerClose is the input to two of six kill criteria and nothing in either repo produces it.**
  - data/queue.json checkpoints CP-60 killIf 'the specimen close records deskMinutesPerClose > 60' and CP-90 'measured deskMinutesPerClose > 90', both `decidedBy: measurement`. Q-030's doneWhen does not mention it. `docs/handoff/bones/board-service-delivery.md:36` states review_started_at/reviewed_at and prepared_at/signed_at/delivered_at 'exist nowhere in apps/api/src'. No queue item adds them; `grep deskMinutes scripts/*.mjs` returns nothing.
  - Cost: The number that decides whether the ~40-location ceiling (and therefore the entire $600 × 40 revenue argument) is real cannot be computed at either checkpoint. A measurement-decided kill criterion with no producer will be resolved by judgement and will resolve favorably, which is the failure mode checkpoints exist to prevent.
- **[MAJOR] Founder review overhead is priced as a flat 2.5h/month constant while agent output varies 3x across windows and 25 of 46 items carry founderHours 0.**
  - data/queue.json#capacity.reviewOverheadPerMonth = 2.5h, described as covering 'PR review and the two production deploys'. Summing `effort` on agent-owned items: M1 12.25h, M2 37.0h, M3 23.0h = 72.25 agent-hours. M2 alone puts 37 agent-hours — including all five correctness fixes to the arithmetic being sold — through 2.5h of review. `board-execution-feasibility.md:42` records that the founder is already not reviewing at agent throughput ('PR #536 merged with three reds accepted') and recommends capping PRs at one item and redefining review as reading the verify output; no queue item adopts it.
  - Cost: Either the correctness fixes ship unreviewed — into the one arithmetic the company signs its name to — or review consumes founder hours the budget does not contain, and the whole 'agent hours are free' premise the 39.25h total rests on is unfunded.
- **[MAJOR] Five queue verify commands name scripts that do not exist, including one item in `whatIsNeverDropped` — so doing the work cannot close the item.**
  - Q-014 verify: `node scripts/check-cost-index-snapshot-freshness.mjs` (cwd product); the actual file is `/home/user/Muntin-Invoice-Decoder/scripts/check-cost-index-snapshot-fresh.mjs`. Also missing and created by no item: `scripts/check-limits-register.mjs` (Q-025), `scripts/check-specimen-close.mjs` (Q-030), `scripts/check-pack-check-tool.mjs` (Q-032), `scripts/build-refusals-page.mjs` and `scripts/build-freshness-page.mjs` (Q-033). Q-014 is named in `capacity.floorPlan.whatIsNeverDropped`.
  - Cost: `--done` refuses to close on a failing verify, so founder hours are spent and the item stays open — the exact defect board-execution-feasibility.md:6 found on Q-006 ('the item cannot be closed by doing it'), reproduced in four more items and in one the plan says can never be dropped.
- **[MAJOR] The queue gate — the single mechanism enforcing the budget, the checkpoints and the kill criteria — does not execute today, and the hook hides it.**
  - `node scripts/check-queue.mjs --budget` (and --brief, --checkpoints) exits with 'the queue is malformed (1): Q-004: status must be one of ready|claimed|blocked|done'. Q-004's status is `closed`. `.claude/hooks/session-start.sh` calls it as `node scripts/check-queue.mjs --brief 2>/dev/null || true`, so a session sees silence rather than the failure.
  - Cost: On day 0 of the ninety days, --budget cannot sum the hours, --checkpoints cannot fire, and the session-start hook — the fix for '1 of 108 transcripts carried a CLAUDE.md' — points sessions at nothing. Every capacity claim in this plan is currently unenforced.
- **[MODERATE] Acquisition and onboarding hours are priced only for the conversation itself, not for sourcing, screening, or the 60-75 day path to a first correct close.**
  - Q-041 = 6 founderHours for 8 *qualified* conversations (verify requires vendorCount ≥ 8 and 1-3 locations per entry) — 45 min each with zero minutes for finding, screening, scheduling, no-shows or follow-up; its `why` states '45 minutes each is the whole day-90 acquisition budget'. Q-042 = 2h for three signings plus one delivered signed close plus one invoice, with no onboarding line. board-service-delivery.md:6 puts time-to-first-correct-close at 60-75 days because the baseline count sheet is built only from purchases inside the window, so a real Beginning leg needs a full prior purchase month.
  - Cost: A customer signed inside M3 cannot produce a correct close inside M3, so Q-042's 'one signed close delivered' resolves to the specimen or to a close whose Beginning leg is not real — and the day-90 proof point is met by an artifact rather than a customer.
- **[MODERATE] Bus factor is not addressed by a single queue item, and CP-90 success is the event that makes it customer-facing.**
  - `grep -oic` on data/queue.json: succession 0, continuity 0, insurance 0, CODEOWNERS 0; ADR-033 likewise. The audit finding exists — `docs/handoff/company-audit-2026-08-07/findings.json:2665` 'bus-factor-absolute' — and `lens-premortem.md:137` scopes the remedy at one day (GitHub org, sealed credential inventory across ~20 named secrets, one named reachable human, run the drill). `runbooks/backup-restore-drill.md:65-67`: 'if the KMS key is lost, ciphertext is unrecoverable', against a drill logged as never executed.
  - Cost: At CP-90 three restaurants are paying and their encrypted invoice archive is one lost 2FA device from permanent destruction, with no second human able to rotate a secret or answer security@. A CPA asked to recommend a one-person vendor will ask this question, and Q-031 puts two CPAs in front of the artifact at CP-60 with no answer prepared.
- **[MODERATE] The floor drop-order buys 4h of which 2h is choosing the optimistic end of an estimate for a task never performed.**
  - capacity.floorPlan.dropOrderToReach13h: Q-006 (−0.5), Q-009 (−0.5, by taking the retire fork), Q-010 (−1.0, deferred), then 'Q-001 (−2.0h) lands at its 4h lower bound rather than the 6h ceiling it is budgeted at'. Q-001 is the founder running his own product end-to-end for the first time in ~15 months; its own effort field is '4-6h'.
  - Cost: Only 1.5h of the 4h is an actual deferral of scope. If M1 needs the drop order and Q-001 runs long — the likely outcome for a first-ever walk through a pipeline with four known open correctness defects — M1 lands back above the floor with the drop order already spent.
- **[MINOR] The plan-of-record's own summary block still states the scope the capacity cut removed.**
  - data/queue.json#strategy.phases.P3 = 'Weeks 10-13. The price call, the application page, ten locations, hand-invoiced', while capacity.cuts states 'TEN locations by day 90 becomes THREE signed locations' and Q-042's doneWhen requires 3.
  - Cost: Small now, load-bearing later: a future session reading `strategy.phases` — the block named 'the strategy of record' — will size M3 against ten locations and re-derive a sales budget that does not exist.
