## Thesis
The plan does not update the privacy corpus for the service relationship at all — it does not touch it. Across all 46 items in `data/queue.json`, the strings `dpa.md`, `ropa.md`, `sub-processors.md` and `dpia` appear exactly zero times (verified by grep), and `DPIA`, `GDPR`, `sub-processor`, `PII` and `processor` return zero case-insensitive matches in the whole file. The board's disqualifying finding — that the agent-run exception desk makes Anthropic an undisclosed sub-processor and falsifies the company's load-bearing claim — has been answered with one subordinate clause inside Q-072's `why` field ("the plain-language disclosure that the exception desk produces RULES and never numbers", `data/queue.json:1231`), which is literally the remedy the board named as insufficient: prose on `/close/` and nowhere in the DPA, the sub-processor list, or the ROPA. Meanwhile `docs/sub-processors.md:43` still tells customers "The deterministic v4 architecture removed the LLM sub-processor (Anthropic) entirely; no third party processes invoice content," and `docs/ropa.md:68` still records "No LLM provider is in the customer-data path." Worse, the company's sole automated proof of that claim, `scripts/no-llm-ci.sh`, is a code-import scanner — it is structurally incapable of detecting an agent session reading a failed invoice outside the repo, and `docs/marketing-claims.md:26` cites it as the evidence for a claim about data, not code. Underneath the desk question sits a second, quieter mismatch nobody has raised: the DPA and ROPA describe a processor running self-serve software with the CUSTOMER's operator in the loop (`docs/dpa.md:286-289`, `docs/ropa.md:69`), while the plan sells a statement Muntin itself produces and signs — a processing purpose that has no ROPA activity, no legal basis, no retention entry, and no DPIA material-change log line, when the DPIA's own §5 names exactly this class of change as a review trigger. The single most schedule-critical omission: `docs/sub-processors.md:22-24` promises that once paying customers exist any sub-processor addition restarts a full 30-day clock "with no founder override available," and nothing in M1/M2/M3 starts that clock before Q-042 signs three locations. The most important thing still missing is a founder ruling, in writing, on whether an agent session will ever see invoice VALUES — because four of my blocking gaps are downstream of that one unanswered question, and the plan asks it nowhere.
