## Thesis
The plan is honest about acquisition in a way almost no plan is: it abolishes traffic, declares that at forty customers qualification IS the funnel, budgets M3 almost entirely to acquisition (8 of 8.5 founder-hours sit in Q-041 + Q-042), and writes a real falsifier — eight qualified conversations, zero signed, stop building. That is the right shape. But the plan records a conversation quota and never builds the thing that produces conversations. Across all 46 queue items the words outreach, network, cold and introduction appear zero times; prospect appears once and referral once, inside a summary string of ADR-014. The board's DISQUALIFYING finding — "no acquisition mechanism for the first ten customers" — was acknowledged and not closed: its own recommendation, name the first ten by name from the DMV history with a vendor-mix and employer-conflict screen in Phase 0, exists in no item. The consequence is worse than a missing to-do: CP-90's kill criteria both presuppose that eight conversations happened, so the most likely failure — the pipeline never fills — reds a checkpoint with no decision rule attached, and the plan cannot distinguish "the buyer said no" from "I never reached a buyer." Two cheap fixes sit unclaimed. Q-002 buys employer authorization to PROCESS invoice data but never asks whether Don's Tacombi employment permits SELLING a food-cost service to DMV independents — the same conversation, the same hour, and it gates the only warm channel the company has. And /try (apps/web/app/(marketing)/try, ADR-007) — a stranger drops his own invoice into the deterministic engine, no persistence, already built and hardened — appears zero times in the queue and is linked from zero of 1,327 storefront pages, while Q-032 budgets a fresh 6h build of a weaker falsifier. On sizing: the storefront is not right-sized, but not mainly because it is too big. 406 pages stay maintained (306 of them Cost Index) with no stated funnel job, /close/ ships with no reachability requirement, and 547 product CTAs across 405 files point at a hostname the product repo does not name as the app. The most important thing still missing is the list of names.
