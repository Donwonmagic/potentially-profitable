# Disposition of the estate — what happens to everything already built (shells, product surfaces, tools, sheets, Spanish, audio, and the meaning of "freeze")

**Verdict: MAJOR_REVISION**

## Thesis
The strategy is sound about what to sell and silent about what it is standing on, and the silence hides one structural impossibility. "An agent-run exception desk under one hard constraint" is the delivery mechanism the whole $600 price rests on, and there is no seat for it anywhere in either repo: `/ops` is gated by `requireFounder` and its own docstring says responses "NEVER include invoice content or extracted document text," `/inbox` is customer-org-scoped under per-request RLS, and a grep for impersonate / support_access / actAs / onBehalf across `apps/api/src` and `apps/web/lib` returns nothing. The desk cannot see the invoice it is supposed to write a rule about. Beneath that sits a positioning contradiction the verdict never names: roughly 4,400 lines of E2EE, BYOK, document-DEK, erasure and recovery-crypto code, plus Swift and Rust parity implementations with their own macOS CI lanes and a 557-line `/promises` page, exist to guarantee Muntin cannot read customer invoices — and the new product is Muntin reading customer invoices and signing the result. Second, the verdict's central disposal verb does not exist as a mechanism: 261 pages are already "frozen," yet 237 still carry inbound links, 249 still carry a subscribe form, 90 still emit analytics, 49 still carry audio, and 5 of 133 `check-*.mjs` scripts are noindex-aware — and the freeze the positioning gate accepts (`noindex, follow`, 209 of 261 pages) is not the freeze `check-locale-parity.mjs:173` honors (`noindex` + `nofollow`). Freezing 460 pages as written changes a meta tag and cancels no obligation. Third, the surface census is roughly four times the brief: 54 pages under `apps/web/app/(product)` and a second, sitemapped, bilingual 37-page marketing site living inside the product repo — while `/close/`, `/close/specimen/`, `/close/limits/`, `/close/apply/`, `/cost-index/refusals/`, `/cost-index/freshness/` and `/tools/pack-check/` are zero for seven on disk. Fourth, the Spanish call is inverted: 508 static ES prose pages cost nothing to leave standing, and the 143 `/es/cost-index/` pages the verdict keeps are the only ES surface carrying a permanent freshness obligation — while the product itself is English-only (2 of 161 product files consume the locale bundle against 17 of 37 marketing pages). Fifth, "freeze the audio manifest, add nothing" leaves 105 of 105 tracks without a contentHash, so no gate can ever detect that an MP3 is still narrating a number the corrections program retracted, and the two flagship dispatch pages already render a Listen button with zero tracks. Finally, the retirement bill nobody costed: 107 storefront pages carry `$19`, 135 carry `$25`/`$60`, 402 link `ledger.muntin.digital`, and `apps/web/app/robots.ts` and `sitemap.ts` both default `SITE_URL` to that same dead host — which is what a CPA hits in the first two minutes of the forty the strategy is banking on. Adopt the spine; the estate plan needs to be written, and one founder question has to be answered before any of it can be.

## Neglected

  - Evidence: `apps/api/src/routes/ops.ts` mounts `requireFounder` on every route and its header states responses "NEVER include invoice content or extracted document text"; `apps/api/src/middleware/require-founder.ts` is a strict FOUNDER_EMAIL equality check with no org dimension. `/inbox` (`apps/web/app/(product)/inbox/page.tsx`) fetches `?status=needs_review` inside one RLS-resolved org. grep for `impersonat|support_access|actAs|onBehalf` across `apps/api/src` and `apps/web/lib` returns zero hits. Separately: `find 'app/(product)' -name page.tsx | wc -l` = 54 (the brief lists 13), plus 37 pages under `app/(marketing)` inside the PRODUCT repo with their own `robots.ts`/`sitemap.ts`. And `/close/`, `/close/specimen/`, `/close/limits/`, `/close/apply/`, `/cost-index/refusals/`, `/cost-index/freshness/`, `/tools/pack-check/` — 0 of 7 exist on disk (only `cost-index/corrections/` does).

- **[DISQUALIFYING] The privacy architecture — the largest single body of built work outside extraction — is a structural blocker under a hand-delivered close, and the plan disposes of neither the code nor the public promise.**

## Recommendations
- [founder] **Answer read/no-read before disposing of a single file. One sentence from the founder: does Muntin's staff (human or agent) read customer invoice content to resolve exceptions? Write it as an ADR in `docs/security/decisions/` the same day.** — It is the hinge for ~4,400 LOC of E2EE/BYOK/recovery code, the Swift and Rust parity packages and their macOS CI lanes, `/promises`, five settings sub-surfaces, and whether the exception desk is buildable at all. Every keep/freeze/delete below flips on it, and the plan currently assumes both answers at once.
- [agent] **Build the vendor-side seat as a customer-granted, time-boxed, audited capability — or change the delivery model. Reuse the shape already proven in `apps/api/src/routes/accountant-handoffs.ts`: token-addressed, TTL-bound, single-use, revocable, audit-chained — but inverted (the customer grants Muntin scoped read on one close). Do not add a standing vendor role or a cross-org RLS bypass.** — There is no impersonation or support-access path anywhere today and `/ops` is content-blind by design, so the exception desk has no seat. A per-close consent grant is the only version that survives the honesty fence and gives the close receipt something true to say about who looked at what.
- [agent] **Define ONE freeze primitive and ship it before freezing anything: `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, follow">` plus a `<!-- muntin:frozen asOf=… reason=… -->` sentinel, a shared `scripts/lib/frozen.mjs` exporting `isFrozen(path)`, and an import of it in every content gate (article-graphics, audio-coverage, locale-parity, claim freshness, positioning-drift). The same pass strips the subscribe form and the analytics init from frozen pages. Add `check-freeze-coverage.mjs` asserting that a frozen page carries no live form, no analytics, and no track.** — Today 5 of 133 gates can see the state, `check-locale-parity.mjs:173` honors a different directive than `check-positioning-drift` demands, and 249 of 261 already-frozen pages still collect email. Without this, freezing 460 pages retires zero hours and the plan's capacity relief is fictional.
- [agent] **Shells: keep `api`, `web`, `email-worker`. Freeze `mobile` — keep the code, disable the `ios-build` and `ios-crypto-parity` macOS lanes in `.github/workflows/mobile.yml`. Reduce `apps/ios` to a parity-vectors package (or delete, per the read/no-read call). Delete `apps/desktop`'s distribution claims immediately regardless of the shell's fate: its README advertises `muntin.digital/ledger/download` and a Sparkle updater against a storefront `ledger/` directory that has no download route.** — macOS runners are the repo's most expensive recurring CI cost and they gate nothing that ships to forty hand-delivered customers. An unsigned WebView advertising an auto-updater against a nonexistent endpoint is a promise the brand cannot afford; withdrawing the claim costs one commit and keeps the option.
- [agent] **Classify all 54 product surfaces on one axis — customer-needs-it / desk-needs-it / neither — and put "neither" into a dated DORMANT registry rather than deleting. Customer-load-bearing: `/count`, `/document/[id]/manual`, `/settings/members`. Desk-internal: `/inbox`, `/insights/learning`, `/insights/drift`, `/bookkeeper/*`, `/ops`. Dormant until a close exists: `/today`, `/recipes`, `/reorder`, `/insights/menu-margin`, `/insights/use-tax`, `/settings/labor`, `/settings/budgets`, `/integrations`. Each dormant entry names the condition that revives it.** — The verdict named 13 surfaces and there are 54; deletion is irreversible and the pilot has not run, but leaving them in nav contradicts "the deliverable is a signed close, not a login." The registry shape is already proven here — `check-idem-coverage.mjs`'s MANUAL registry is the one place this company has successfully forced a retirement.
- [agent] **Retire the audio corpus by rule, re-render nothing: delete the 40 `course/*` entries from `data/article-audio.json`; strip the Listen UI from the 42 pages that render it with zero tracks, starting with `/blog/cost-index-2026-07/` and `/blog/cost-index-week-2026-07-06/`; add to the corrections runbook that correcting a page with a track deletes the track; then flip `check-audio-coverage.mjs` from `--warn` to fail on MISSING-ARTICLE and listen-UI-without-track only.** — All 105 tracks lack a contentHash, so nothing on disk can detect an MP3 narrating a retracted number — and Phase 0 is about to retract 72 of them on a page that displays a Listen button. Deleting a track is honest; a frozen corpus with a blind gate is the fabrication risk the audio pipeline was always going to produce.
- [founder] **Invert the Spanish disposition: leave the ~508 static ES prose pages standing and frozen, and drop the parity/freshness ratchet on `/es/cost-index/` instead — publishing an EN-only index with a stated reason. In the same commit, state on the record that the product is English-only (2 of 161 product files consume the locale bundle) and remove the Spanish 24-hour reply and phone-callback SLA from `copy.es.ts`.** — Static prose generates no recurring obligation; 143 dated ES data pages generate one forever. The verdict kept the only ES surface that costs money to keep and froze the half that was already free — and a Spanish reader who converts today reaches an English-only application, which is a promise gap, not a translation gap.
- [agent] **Pay the link bill in Phase 0 alongside the number bill: one scripted pass repointing 402 pages off `ledger.muntin.digital`, one pass replacing `$19`/`$25`/`$60` across 107 + 135 pages (plus ES mirrors and `copy.ts`/`copy.es.ts`) with "Pricing set at the close of the pilot," set `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` in the web deploy so `robots.ts` and `sitemap.ts` stop emitting the dead host, and either re-sign `docs/warrant-canary.md` on a cadence the founder will actually keep or retire it with a dated note and fix the three public references in `legal-content.generated.ts`.** — The storefront's new job is surviving forty minutes of a CPA's scrutiny. A dead "start your ledger" button, a stale price, and a warrant canary two months past its own stated cadence are all found faster than a corrected BLS basis — and each is a scripted pass, not a judgement call.

## Would change my verdict
Whether Muntin's staff — human or agent — reads customer invoice content to resolve exceptions. It is one sentence from the founder and it is also free to obtain from the two CPA/bookkeeper conversations already scheduled in Phase 2: ask "would your engagement letter permit a vendor whose staff reads the client's AP to resolve exceptions, and what disclosure would you require?" If the answer is no-read, the agent-run exception desk dies, the ~4,400 LOC of E2EE/BYOK/recovery code and the Swift and Rust parity packages become the moat and every shell stays, and the $600 must be justified by the count-shrinking machinery alone. If the answer is yes-read, `/promises` comes down, the privacy code and the crypto shells become the largest deletable asset in either repo, and the top priority becomes building a consent-gated vendor seat that does not exist today. Nearly every recommendation above resolves differently under the two branches, and the plan as written silently assumes both.
