# The Cost Index Data Asset

**NOT_READY**

## Thesis
The plan never states what the Cost Index is FOR, and every gap below follows from that omission. The board's core finding — the Index is 42-47% of the obligation ledger with no revenue mechanism — is answered nowhere in `data/queue.json` (46 items, 8 cost-index-related, none about scope, role, or retirement) and only obliquely in ADR-032, which keeps "the 306 Cost Index pages stay fresh" as the single retained ratchet on the premise that "their data cadence is automated, not founder-paid" — a premise `check-cost-index-series-freshness.mjs` falsifies on 26 of 100 series today. Yes, the minimum viable honest Index is far smaller than 100: the product consumes exactly 24 slugs (`apps/api/src/data/cost-index-snapshot.json`), those 24 are 13 medium / 11 low / 0 high confidence and are disjoint from all 26 frozen series, and the other 76 feed no product path. The plan's most serious unaddressed defect is that the flagship composite is not reproducible from its own file: `data/cost-index.json#basket.pct` is byte-identical to `contributors[0].pct` (chicken-breast), and recomputing the same weighted basket from the file's own live `points[0].trend.pct` gives +1.98% against a published −0.5% — an opposite sign, rendered at `cost-index/index.html:674` and in the ES mirror, exported CC0 inside a `DataCatalog` JSON-LD. No gate recomputes it (`check-cost-index-sync.mjs:179-190` type-checks only). Under the plan's own doctrine — pay the honesty debt IN FULL before amplifying — Phase 0 corrects 72 dispatch claims and one feed price while leaving the largest published number unreproducible. Second: ADR-033 §4's "two hard dates the plan would otherwise walk into" omits the biggest one — the 120-day cliff drops 17 ingredients in a single build on or about 2026-09-29 and one more on 2026-10-06, CP-60's own due date, removing 3 of 16 basket contributors carrying 19% of declared weight, unattended, during the specimen-close and CPA-review window. Third: the one question that decides whether the Index has any product function — `marketPriorAcceptable` — sits behind a seven-item dependency chain in M2, after every hour of Index work is spent. The plan is close: real teeth, dated checkpoints, a genuine kill criterion on the market prior. It is not ready because it spends on an asset whose job is undeclared, publishes a headline it cannot reproduce, and walks into a dated 17-ingredient content event no item anticipates.
