## Thesis
The plan is an outstanding execution instrument and a poor strategy document, and the two are being confused. On my domain it is close to silent: "MarginEdge" appears zero times in `data/queue.json` and zero times in ADR-030/031/032/033, so no item, checkpoint or kill criterion in the ninety days anticipates an incumbent response, a copy, or a price move. The one stated differentiator — "a fixed monthly close date is a promise their structure cannot make" (`docs/editorial/decisions/ADR-030-one-price-one-cohort-no-billing-code.md:75`) — was found disqualifying by the board (`docs/handoff/bones/board-service-delivery.md:35`, `board-completeness.md:26`) and killed, and nothing in the queue replaces it; ADR-030 still ships the sentence. What remains as the moat claim is the vendor-mix wedge, and its evidence does not support the claim being made. The 84-87% straight-through figure is measured on ONE org's mature corpus (`services/extract/VENDOR-TEMPLATE-KEY.md:203-236`), under a trust model keyed to `(org, vendor, header-signature)` that requires 3-4 prior confirmations and ships in SHADOW by default (`services/extract/main.py:112-120`); the same file's own table says the cold state is 24% (`VENDOR-TEMPLATE-KEY.md:17-27`). Nobody has measured what a NEW org gets on day one, and the entire validation loop — Q-030 specimen, Q-031 CPA review, CP-60 — runs on the one org where that number is already high. Two of the three checkpoints are load-bearing on `deskMinutesPerClose` (`data/queue.json:1613,1662`), and nothing named `deskMinutes`/`desk_minutes`/`close_minutes` exists anywhere in the product repo, with no queue item to create it: a falsifier that cannot be measured is not a falsifier. Meanwhile the product's own 37 live marketing pages sell a different company — six `/vs/` comparison pages last fact-checked 2026-05-21 (`apps/web/app/(marketing)/vs/marginedge/page.tsx:56`) that position Muntin as a subset of the incumbent ("Muntin Ledger does the first part. We do not yet do the rest.") — and `data/surface-disposition.json` dispositions zero of them. The most important thing still missing is a measured cold-start reconcile rate for an org with zero prior confirmations; without it, the wedge, the $600 price and the 40-location ceiling all rest on Tacombi's maturity rather than on anything portable.
