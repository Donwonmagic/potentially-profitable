## Thesis
The financial work exists and is good — `docs/handoff/bones/finance-model.{md,json,py}` is the most rigorous artifact in the material — but it was not absorbed into the plan. Of the eight recommendations in `bones/board-financial-model.md`, two were partially taken (annual prepay → monthly hand-invoice, `data/queue.json:1482`) and six have no queue item at all: `grep -i` over `data/queue.json` returns zero hits for insurance, E&O, counsel, collection, subscription, break-even or self-sustaining. The plan gates hours with real teeth (`capacity.founderHoursPerMonth`, `check-queue.mjs --budget`, a written `floorPlan` drop order) and gates dollars not at all — there is no `cashUsd` field, no `capacity.cashBudget`, and no declared source of the ~$9,300 the model says leaves the bank across M1-M3 at zero revenue (`finance-model.md` §3). The parameter the whole business ranges 1,000x on — `deskMinutesPerClose`, residual $224/yr to $280,002/yr at the service ceiling (§5) — is still not scheduled to be measured: Q-001's founder walk records four numbers and none of them is it, and CP-60's kill test names a producer, `scripts/check-specimen-close.mjs`, that does not exist on disk. Most seriously, M2 publishes a specimen close and M3 delivers a **signed** statement of food cost and a $600 invoice with no item for E&O, counsel, or entity confirmation anywhere in the dependency graph — Q-042's `blockedBy` is `["Q-041"]` — while the company's two live legal documents contradict each other on home state (`terms.html:423` Maryland vs `Muntin-Invoice-Decoder/docs/tos.md:135` Delaware), the live ToS disclaims the reliance the $600 buys (`docs/tos.md:99`), and `docs/legal-counsel-kickoff.md:353-356` still asks for "confirmation an entity EXISTS ... it gates everything." On self-sustaining: infra break-even is 1-5 locations and therefore meaningless; the real threshold is the year-one legal/insurance apparatus, which pushes break-even above the plan's own day-90 target of three locations. Founder time is the binding cost from day zero, not at some customer count — at the 13h floor the plan explicitly sizes against, the model's own BASE parameters serve roughly ten locations, a quarter of the "~40 locations ever" the strategy states at `data/queue.json:23`. The most important thing missing is not an analysis; it is a dollar column with a gate on it, sitting next to the hour column that already has one.

- **[MAJOR] The strategy states "~40 locations ever" while the plan explicitly sizes capacity against the 13h floor. Those are not the same business.**
