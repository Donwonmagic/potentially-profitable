# The Completeness Seat — what no seat at this table covers

**Verdict: MAJOR_REVISION**

## Thesis
The spine is right and I am not attacking it: correctness before amplification, one price, a hard-capped cohort, employer authorization, no billing code, and the founder's own end-to-end walk in week one are all correct, and the reasoning that got there is the best thinking in this engagement. What the judge produced is a superb product-correctness plan wearing a company's clothes. Three of its own load-bearing words each require an artifact that does not exist and that no phase creates: "signed" needs an engagement letter and a liability position, "audit opinion" needs a licensing and reliance answer, and "hand-invoiced" needs an entity, a tax determination, and a refund policy that is not the retired studio's. The live public `terms.html` still governs a web-design studio — "if you hire the studio, the engagement letter governs," "the studio runs two builds at a time," Maryland law — and `check-positioning-drift.mjs` passes it at 0 violations because its vocabulary list hunts "wordpress" and "wix," not "studio," "proposal," and "care plan"; the gate the founder is proudest of cannot see the company's binding legal instrument. More seriously, the plan has no route to customer one. Across the whole verdict the words network, outbound, introduction, and cold appear zero times; acquisition is "an application page" and "~400 qualified readers" who arrive by no named mechanism, while `sourced-claims.json` records four DMV restaurants the founder has personally worked in — the only warm channel this company has, unnamed and unscreened for the employer conflict that makes it dangerous. Two structural questions went unasked by 100+ agents: there is no labor plan (succession appears zero times; "first hire" appears once, to dismiss it), so the $288K ceiling is a hiring decision presented as a physical constant; and there is no continuity plan, so ten customers who prepay $60,000 against a ToS promising pro-rated refunds are unsecured creditors of a man with a day job. Finally, and most likely to kill the plan on contact: the verdict never cites `docs/handoff/founder-vision.md`, where on 2026-07-11 the founder decided in his own words to *stop* foregrounding that it is one person — "I worry that if people know it's just me, they'll discount the product" — and this strategy makes the one person the product, signs his name to the number, and proposes to publish "a very good one-person business" as positioning. That is not a detail; it is the last recorded founder decision, silently overturned. Adopt the spine, revise the plan.
