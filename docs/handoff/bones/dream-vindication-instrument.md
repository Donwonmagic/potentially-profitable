# THE VINDICATION INSTRUMENT — Muntin stops selling software and starts issuing evidence. The company's product is a named, dated, cited, printable object that an operator carries into a room and puts on the table in front of a vendor rep, and every other surface exists to make that object survive being checked. Suspicion → proof → standing. Never prediction.

## Thesis
Every incumbent in this category sells relief from work; nobody sells standing in an argument, and standing is the only thing a working operator actually loses in public. The buyer is not optimizing a percentage at 10pm — he is sitting across a table from a professional who prices for a living, who calls on dozens of restaurants a week, and who has better information than he does. Muntin is structurally the only company that can arm him, because vindication requires evidence that a third party has to accept, and Muntin's entire balance sheet is dated, public, CC0, methodologically-published evidence with a 10,294-entry append-only revisions ledger behind it. The instrument is not a dashboard and not a forecast — the 2026-07 rigor audit permanently killed direction claims ("the band is HOW FAR, never WHICH WAY"), which is not a limitation here but the specification: a sheet that says *this is what the public market did, dated, sourced, verify it yourself* is unloseable in a way a prediction never is. The remarkable finding is that Muntin already built this and never assembled it: `assets/site.css:1663` already prints every `<details class="cite">` drawer with its source URL appended in parentheses, `assets/sheets.css` already carries a print-first "stay-paper" artifact system, `brand/mark/` and `brand/lockup/` already ship 13 SVGs, and `tools/_shared/` holds 42 fully-written, parity-locked, EN+ES honesty-gated engines — including `pack-shrink.js`, `vendor-switch.js`, `silent-bleed.js` and `url-fragment.js` — that are referenced by exactly zero HTML pages on a 1,327-page site. The company built the printing press, the paper stock, the watermark and the type, and never printed a page. Distribution follows for free and follows from nothing else this company has ever had: the rep who sees the sheet calls on twenty to sixty restaurants, reads the URL in the footer, and becomes an unpaid channel in a room Muntin did not pay to enter — the only acquisition arithmetic that closes at any price. It also resolves the company's oldest deadlock, "own the number or own the noun," in a third direction that costs no permanent founder call: own the OBJECT. Kelley Blue Book is not a number and not a category label; it is a printout a person holds on a car lot. Under this bet the free instrument is the business's engine and Muntin Ledger becomes what it always should have been — the machine that produces the sheet automatically from your own invoices and keeps the archive that makes next year's argument stronger than this year's.

## The Product
WHAT MUNTIN LEDGER BECOMES: not a food-cost dashboard, but **the machine that issues your sheet automatically and keeps your archive**. Upload the invoices; every line is normalized to `cents_per_base` and reconciled; the product produces (a) the same Market Sheet the free tool makes, but pre-filled with the items you actually buy and annotated with your own delivered move beside the public move, and (b) the ask — the literal sentence, from `vendor-ask.ts`, naming the vendor, the item, the two percentages and the gap. The Milwaukee platform lock is the archive: it is privacy-architected, it is yours, it grows every month, and it is the only source that can answer "is it me or the market" for an item the public index withholds on. WHAT GETS BUILT (in order): the sheet renderer, shared verbatim between the client-side tool and the product; the watch alert; and the vendor-trust flip — `EXTRACT_VENDOR_TRUST_ACTIVE` already holds a measured 24% → ~62% first-pass straight-through improvement on the 919-document corpus and is sitting in shadow mode, so the single largest first-run quality gain in the company is a flag, not a project. WHAT GETS CUT — and this is the hard call this bet makes: **the food-cost identity tier does not launch.** `/insights/food-cost` and `/insights/variance` go behind a flag until the four named defects are paid. They are not small: `line_item_observations` was engineered for price intelligence and reused unchanged as the Purchases leg (it drops credit memos, $0 promo lines, freight and surcharges, and unparseable pack tokens, and stores `unit_price × quantity` rather than printed `line_total`); the default count mode writes un-entered items at expected-on-hand so their usage is arithmetically zero, biasing the headline low; `inventory-purchases-store.ts:19-20` documents that the Purchases roll-up carries no location predicate while counts are per-location, so in Don's own two-location shape each location's usage contains the other's invoices; and `inventory-reconcile.ts:249` is an empty `if (estimated) {}` block whose comment claims behavior the code does not perform. A number that is biased three ways is exactly the number an instrument brand cannot print. Deferring it costs us nothing under this bet, because the sheet's claim never touches it. HOW CORRECTNESS IS GUARANTEED — the bet narrows the correctness surface to something achievable and then gates it absolutely. The sheet makes exactly one class of claim: *this public wholesale reference, on this date, moved this far from this baseline, and here is the frozen edition and the source series.* It makes NO claim about the operator's landed cost — which is precisely the trap `IS-IT-YOU-OR-THE-MARKET.md` already documents ("that's wholesale, not my landed cost" loses the argument). The operator supplies his invoice number; the sheet supplies the standing. Enforcement is a new gate, `check-sheet-provenance.mjs`: every number that can appear on a printed sheet must resolve to a frozen `asOf` snapshot in `data/cost-index-editions.json`, never a live file. That gate is also the fix for the disease that produced 72 published falsehoods on the flagship — the `<!-- src: -->` convention carrying that post's entire numeric load is a parallel, machine-unenforced system operating outside the fact gate, and the sheet is the forcing function that finally makes provenance a build-time property rather than an editorial habit. One-in-one-out: it enters only as 320 checks come down. And the determinism guarantee stops being a footnote — `no-llm-ci.sh` is falsifiable by a stranger in thirty seconds against a public repo, is named on zero of 1,327 pages today, and belongs printed on the sheet itself: *no language model reads your numbers; here is the command that proves it.*

## How It Fails
THE FAILURE I WOULD BET ON: the rep's first sentence wins. The sheet carries public wholesale references; the rep sells delivered cost, and his rehearsed reply is "that's the terminal market — it doesn't include freight, fuel, my service level, or your allocation during a shortage." That objection is TRUE, and `IS-IT-YOU-OR-THE-MARKET.md` already documents it as the thing that loses the argument. I have designed the sheet to survive it — it claims only what the public market did, dated and sourced, and the operator supplies his own invoice number — but design intent is not what happens at a table. If the rep deflects successfully and the operator walks away feeling naive rather than vindicated, the instrument is strictly worse than nothing: it made him look amateur in front of a professional, which is the exact inverse of the promise, and he will never bring it again. Vindication is high-stakes in a way relief is not; a tool that loses one argument burns permanently. THE SECOND FAILURE: the emotion may not be there. Vindication is felt by people who are willing to fight. A large share of independent operators would rather keep the rep friendly — he controls substitutions, credit terms, and who gets product during a shortage — and will judge that the relationship is worth more than three points on one line. If that is the median operator, we have built an instrument for a minority temperament and mistaken it for a market. THE THIRD: this bet defers the food-cost number rather than fixing it, which is correct today and becomes a wall the moment paying operators ask for the thing the category trained them to want. THE FOURTH: the sheet is visually copyable in an afternoon. Its only real defense is the archive, the cadence and the verification surface — and the cadence is already breaking, with 26 of 100 series frozen 58–77 days against a 120-day cliff. EARLY SIGNALS THAT PROVE THIS WRONG, in order of speed: (1) Don's first two rep conversations, in weeks 3–4, at zero cost. If both reps deflect to landed cost and the exchange dies there, the bet is falsified in a month instead of a year — and that verbatim quote is worth more than everything else in this plan. (2) The print rate on `/market-sheet/`. If people build sheets and do not print or save them, the object is not an object; it is another dashboard, and the room it was designed for does not exist. (3) Whether anyone ever types a URL off a printed footer. If `/methods/`, `/no-llm/` and `/corrections/` get no traffic from sheet holders after ninety days, then nobody is checking, which means nobody needed proof, which means we sold standing to people who already had it or never wanted it. WHAT WOULD NOT PROVE IT WRONG, and must not be mistaken for failure: low sheet volume in the first sixty days. The distribution mechanic is a rep seeing paper in a room, and that propagates at the speed of a sales route, not a launch.
