The read

Month-end is where most operators find out. That's the wrong window.

Costs don't jump — they drift, a line at a time, under a week already too busy to notice. The P&L just confirms what already happened. Muntin moves that moment earlier, to where you can still do something about it.

How it goes now

You close the month, the food-cost line is up two points, and you spend an afternoon reverse-engineering why. The onions that jumped in week two are invisible by the time you go looking.

"Where did the margin go?" — asked too late to answer.

How it goes with the window

The week a line moves enough to matter, you get one line — a real number, not a dashboard to go check. Most weeks it says nothing, because most weeks nothing moved. That's the read working, not failing.

"Onions are up 15% — reprice the app." — decided on Tuesday.
