# Muntin storefront — the fully-mapped reinvention plan

Complete-coverage basis: **1,329 pages / ~925K lines**, every surface read in full or known
via its generator (see `site-coverage-ledger.md`). This plan maps *each* surface, so nothing is
generic and nothing is missed.

## I. Who Muntin is (the understanding the plan is built on)

A one-person, product-only **restaurant cost-intelligence** company — Don Goldstein, a working
FOH manager. Three products, one job: help an independent operator **see and command the costs
that move invisibly under them.** The name *muntin* — the strips that divide a sheet of glass
into legible panes — is the whole idea: turn the fog of vendor costs into clear panes you can
see through. The brand's soul is **operator-to-operator honesty**; trust is the *foundation it
stands on, not a flag it waves.* The user arrives reactive, squeezed, skeptical, out of time —
and the site's job is to move them to feeling **a step ahead, in command** (empowerment →
retention → advocacy). The money model: free tools (everyday utility) → the monthly **Operator's
Brief** (the return ritual) → **Muntin Ledger** (the paid product, the deepest habit).

## II. The strategic verdict: PRUNE → REFOCUS → ELEVATE (not a re-skin)

The single biggest finding from reading everything: **the site is a cost-intelligence company
buried under a retired web-agency's content mountain.** Evidence:
- **Library:** 56% retired website/SEO/GBP content, only 26% cost-intelligence (of 39 articles) —
  but 77% score ≥4. *A superb library pointed at the wrong reader. Aim, not rebuild.*
- **Retired-era clusters:** the *frozen* `course` (27pg), `learn` (25pg), `method` (20pg), the
  brand/design **sheets**, and ~half the **glossary** terms — all off the cost funnel.
- **Tools:** genuinely good, but every one dead-ends; a `start`/`audits` "17-tool museum" of
  retired links hangs off them; the retention loop is 90% coded but unwired.
So the reinvention is three moves in order: **prune** the agency-era mass (retire/301/refocus),
**refocus** the SEO on-ramp + IA on cost intelligence, and **elevate** the surviving cost core to
app-grade craft. Re-pigmenting 1,329 pages would just make a confused site prettier.

## III. The design & experience system — "one window, many panes"

**Coherence (the frame):** extend the real app's financial-grade tokens site-wide (slate ramp,
one electric-blue accent `#3b68f5`/`#5b82ff`, Inter + tabular numerics, hairline depth, dark-first)
— aliased onto the storefront tokens so it re-pigments with near-zero structural risk (proven on
`/ledger/demo/`, incr. E/F). **Contrast fix (mandatory):** links use `--accent-text #2a50c8` on
light (`#3b68f5` fails AA); status never by hue alone (CVD → add a glyph). Tabular-nums on **data
surfaces only**, not body prose.

**Distinctiveness (what makes it *Muntin*, not a Linear clone):** two ownable assets, held as
**ambient, not defensive** (the founder's correction — stop making every surface argue our virtue):
1. **The muntin/window-grille as structural grammar** — the mullion as the section/grid divider
   system, not a load animation (`/window/` already has the DOM grammar — export it site-wide).
2. **Honesty made visible, quietly** — the graded miss rate, the *withheld* week-over-week, the
   no-LLM build-gate — as *calm instruments*, not "we're more honest than Toast" walls.

**Personality (no two panes alike):** the frame is one; each surface gets its own register + art
direction, and **warmth returns where it belongs** (killing all warmth = generic cold fintech).
The register map is §V. The emotional axis is always *the operator's experience*, not our defense.

## IV. The retention engine (why they come back)

Grounded truth: **honesty and frequency conflict — honest cost data is mostly boring.** So
retention is not a frequent bare index; it is:
1. **The Operator's Brief** — a **monthly** (cadence of the operator's real decision: P&L close /
   menu review) curated **multi-desk digest** — *Market* (commodity futures that lead the slow
   public index) · *Supply* (weather/disease/recalls) · *Distributor* · *Policy/labor* · *From the
   Floor* (Don) · and eventually the **flywheel desk** (aggregate real Ledger invoice prices — the
   proprietary signal no incumbent has). The index is *one desk*, not the show. There **is** weekly-
   relevant information; it just isn't all public-government data — so weekly is *earnable* once the
   sourcing is semi-automated + the flywheel matures. **Prove it in the August edition** (build the
   format now; source real content at publish). Name: **The Operator's Brief**.
2. **Event-spike alerts** — "a thing *you* buy moved enough to matter" — the real return reflex;
   materiality-gated, magnitude-honest, *sometimes "no change — you're fine."*
3. **The tools** — the everyday, event-triggered utility; the honest **save → watch → alert →
   Brief** loop (90% coded — wire it, don't build it).
4. **The Ledger** — the deepest habit (their own invoices > any public index); the Brief is
   top-of-funnel that feeds it.
Cut the cargo-culted consumer mechanics (streak counter, tribe framing, countdown, continue-where-
you-left-off). **Ruthless scope is itself the strategy** for a one-person shop; the real failure
mode is half-built machinery decaying into staleness (the site already opens on a 10-week-old
anchor — the cardinal sin, fix first).
