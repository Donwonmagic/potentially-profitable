Receipts, not promises

Every claim on this site carries its own receipt.

  1. 01 The fact gate

    An invented number here isn’t edited out. It fails the build.

    Every figure on this site is registered in a public claim ledger — 43 sourced claims, each traced to its document. Sixty-plus check scripts run before every deploy, and the prose is read aloud in English and Spanish, so a fabrication would be spoken, not buried.

  2. 02 The network tab

    Free tools compute in your browser. The proof is the network tab.

    Open DevTools and type — the request list does not grow. A named CI gate, check-tool-no-fetch, fails the build if any tool contacts any URL. Ten verifiable claims, each with an “inspect this” drawer.

  3. 03 The public scorecard

    The Cost Index grades its own forecasts in public.

    Its nominal 80% band held 77% of the time across 79,086 scored weeks and 75 ingredients — misses included, published in the methodology. Unpriced items report absent, with the reason, before the Index reports a guess. The data is public domain (CC0), every number dated to USDA, BLS, or FRED.

Questions we get

Straight answers.

Is the library really free? What’s the catch?

No catch. Every article, tool, research note, and glossary term is free to read, apply, and share. The free tools and the Cost Index run on the same numbers as the paid product — apply them yourself, or let Muntin Ledger do it.

Where should I start?

Two doors. If you want to understand the playing field first, take the 60-minute guided tour — what a restaurant website owes a diner, where the money leaks, the math of fixing it. If you want a tool in your hands now, run the five-question routing form and we’ll point you at the two or three calculators that match what’s actually leaking.

Didn’t Muntin Digital used to build websites?

It did. Muntin Digital started as a one-person web studio for restaurants. It’s now a product company: Muntin Ledger, the public Cost Index, and the free operator tools. The library and the tools stay free; the website-build service is retired.

What is Muntin Ledger?

Muntin Ledger reads your vendor invoices and files them into a searchable ledger. Snap a photo and it lands read, filed, and searchable, with price-hike flags against your own history.

Is my invoice data read by an AI model?

No. Muntin Ledger reads invoices with templates and rules — the bytes never go to a language model, and your data is never sold or pooled without your opt-in.

What’s the Cost Index?

A free, public read of wholesale ingredient prices from USDA, BLS, and FRED — dated, sourced, and refreshed Monday, Wednesday, and Friday. No signup. Every number traces back to the agency that published it.

What’s a “muntin”? And why “The window in.”?

A muntin is the slender wooden strip between panes in a real window — small, quiet, and the reason the whole thing stands up. The window in plays on two senses: the mark on the wall (the muntin/windowpane) and the way in — to understanding your restaurant’s web. Small focus, whole picture.
