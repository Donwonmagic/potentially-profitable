OpeningHoursSpecification

the Schema.org type Google reads to render your hours in search results

RestaurantsSchema
Local SEO & Discovery

A specific Schema.org structured-data type for representing when a place is open. Lives inside a Restaurant (or LocalBusiness) JSON-LD block as one or more entries with dayOfWeek, opens, and closes fields. Holiday overrides use the same type via the parallel specialOpeningHoursSpecification property with validFrom/validThrough dates. The format Google’s Rich Results parser actually consumes — the difference between “hours vary” and a full weekly grid in your search snippet.

Why it matters

The customer searching “is [restaurant] open right now?” doesn't open your website. They glance at the rich snippet and decide. Without an OpeningHoursSpecification block on every page, Google falls back to either “hours vary” or to whatever your Google Business Profile says — and when those two sources disagree, your listing inherits the lower confidence. The fix is a small JSON-LD block in your site’s <head>; the alternative is leaving every “open now” query to chance.

Two correctness traps cost most independents a snippet. First: a service that crosses midnight (5 PM – 1 AM) emitted as a single OpeningHoursSpecification with opens: "17:00" and closes: "01:00" is technically permitted but inconsistent in Google’s parser. The defensive pattern is two consecutive entries (5 PM – 11:59 PM same day, 12:00 AM – 1 AM next day). Second: a closure picked in your calendar but never written into specialOpeningHoursSpecification means Google keeps showing standard hours on Thanksgiving until a customer reports the listing as wrong — soft demotion in local search.

Open Hours generates a complete OpeningHoursSpecification + specialOpeningHoursSpecification block (with the next-day defensive pattern handled correctly) for any week + closure schedule you enter. Validate the output at validator.schema.org before shipping.
