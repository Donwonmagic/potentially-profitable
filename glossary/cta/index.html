Call to action

Example: A Silver Spring taqueria puts one bright 'Order Online' button on its first screen and repeats it after the menu, so a hungry visitor never has to scroll hunting for the one action the page most wants them to take.

CTA

Restaurants
Conversions & Reservations

The specific button or link that asks a visitor to do the next thing — "Reserve a Table", "Order Online", "Book a Class", "Get a Quote". Usually bright, clearly labeled, and repeated at natural moments on the page.

Why it matters

Traffic without a clear next step converts at a fraction of the rate. Every page should have one unmistakable primary CTA — the one action you'd most like a visitor to take — and it should be visible on the first screen, not hidden at the bottom.
