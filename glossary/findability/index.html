Findability

How Google, Apple Maps, and voice assistants understand what your business is and where it is. Without this, customers don't find you in the first place.

Terms

19 definitions.

  • Google Business Profile — GBP, formerly Google My Business
  • Title tag — the page's <title>
  • Meta description
  • Schema markup — JSON-LD, structured data, schema.org
  • Alt text — image descriptions
  • Sitemap — sitemap.xml
  • Google Search Console — GSC
  • Canonical URL — rel=canonical, the "official" address
  • robots.txt
  • Open Graph — og:image, social share cards, link previews
  • Rich results — rich snippets, SERP features
  • NAP consistency — name, address, phone — everywhere the same
  • Hours of operation — the times your restaurant is open, expressed in a way every map and listing service can read
  • Special hours — the override for any day that doesn't follow your usual schedule
  • Holiday hours — the recurring per-date overrides every restaurant has to make every year
  • OpeningHoursSpecification — the Schema.org type Google reads to render your hours in search results
  • Structured data — the machine-readable copy of your site that Google reads
  • Apple Maps & Bing Places
  • Mobile-first indexing
