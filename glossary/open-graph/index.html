Open Graph

og:image, social share cards, link previews

Restaurants
Local SEO & Discovery Brand & Design

A set of meta tags that tell Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, iMessage, and Slack how to preview your link when someone pastes it — which image to show, which headline, which description.

Why it matters

Without Open Graph tags, a shared link shows as a grey box with your domain name, which people ignore. With a good food photo and a sharp headline, that same link becomes a thumbnail ad every time anyone posts about you.
