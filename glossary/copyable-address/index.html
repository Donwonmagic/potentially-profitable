The basics

Copyable address

Restaurants
Conversions & Reservations

Your street address as real, selectable text on the page — not baked into an image or a graphic. Ideally wrapped in a Google Maps link so tapping it opens directions.

Why it matters

If a customer can't long-press and copy your address on their phone, they can't paste it into Maps, Uber, or a text to a friend. Text addresses also feed search engines' local-business index; image addresses don't.
