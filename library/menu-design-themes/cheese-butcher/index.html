Theme story

Butcher-paper kraft with slab serif. Pricing-by-weight column because cheese / charcuterie operators sell by 100g, lb, oz — different number formatting than dish menus.

Inspired by

  • Fromageries with cheese flights
  • Whole-animal butchers with charcuterie programs
