Bagel Grid

Fixed-grid pricing card. Monospaced columns, over-counter energy.

Reviewed by Don Won Magic · Added 2026-05-03

Open this theme in the editor →

Theme story

Monospaced columns mean the price column lines up perfectly without leader dots — exactly what an above-counter pricing card needs. No ornament; the grid IS the typography.

Inspired by

  • NY-style bagel shops with above-counter pricing cards
  • Schmear bars
