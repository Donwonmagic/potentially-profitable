The product · General availability November 13, 2026

Your POS counts the sales. Muntin Ledger reads the bills.

In a restaurant, the sales side is wired tight — every cover, every check. The cost side arrives as a stack of vendor invoices nobody reads until the month is already closed. Muntin Ledger reads them the day they land, files them where you can find them, and tells you when a price moved.

Read, filed, flagged.

Three jobs, none of which should take your evening. Muntin Ledger does the part of margin work that a point-of-sale system never touches: the paper that arrives from your vendors.

Read

Templates and rules, not a model.

Snap or upload a vendor invoice. Templates and rules pull the lines — vendor, item, quantity, price, tax. No language model ever reads your documents; a CI gate blocks anyone from adding one.

File

A ledger you can search.

Every invoice lands filed and searchable — by vendor, by item, by date. Export the whole record to a clean CSV, or post it to QuickBooks Online, whenever you ask.

Flag

Price hikes, against your own history.

A vendor raises a price and Ledger marks it against your own six-month history. The dishes that ingredient touches re-cost the same day — the plate-cost number stays current without a spreadsheet.

What it reads, and what it checks. The ledger files any vendor's invoice — food or beverage, line by line — and flags a hike against your own history no matter what's on the page. The extra step, telling a vendor's raise from a whole market moving, leans on the Cost Index, which today prices wholesale proteins, produce, and dairy — not spirits or wine, and not graded or specialty cuts. Outside that set a raise still files and re-costs; it just isn't yet checked against a market read.

Why a platform can't copy this.

Every promise below is adverse to how the largest vendors make money. That is the whole point — a company funded by a per-order rake or by reselling your data can't write these sentences without indicting its own business.

  • No per-order rake. Muntin takes nothing off your tickets. The incentive to push you to sell or order more isn't in the model, because the model isn't a cut of your sales.
  • No language model in the invoice path. Your numbers never become training data. The ban isn't a privacy paragraph — it's a build gate that fails the release if anyone tries to add one.
  • Your data stays yours. Export the whole ledger anytime in a format you can open without us. Delete it and it's gone.
  • Pricing posted in writing. The number is on the page, with the per-invoice cost math next to it. No call, no quote, no “contact sales.”
