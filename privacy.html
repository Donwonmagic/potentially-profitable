Privacy Policy

Effective April 2026

Muntin Digital™ ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your personal information when you visit our website, use our services, or interact with us.

Information We Collect

We collect information in a few different ways:

  • From your inquiries: When you submit our intake form or contact us via email, we collect your name, email address, company information, project details, and any other information you choose to share with us.
  • From scheduling: When you book a meeting through Calendly, we receive your name, email, and calendar information related to the meeting.
  • Website analytics: We use Google Analytics to track how you interact with our website, including pages visited, time spent, device type, and general location (country/region level). No personally identifying information is tracked through analytics.
  • Cookies: Our website uses cookies to remember your preferences and improve your experience. See the "Cookies & Tracking" section below for details.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for legitimate business purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide the services you've requested
  • To schedule and conduct meetings or consultations
  • To send you relevant updates about our services (only if you've opted in)
  • To understand how people use our website and improve its design and functionality
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect against fraud or misuse

Third-Party Services

We use the following trusted third-party services to deliver our offerings. These services have their own privacy policies:

  • Formspree: We use Formspree to securely collect form submissions from our intake form. Formspree handles your submitted data according to their privacy policy.
  • Calendly: When you book a meeting, Calendly receives your booking information. See Calendly's privacy policy for details on how they handle your data.
  • Google Analytics: We use Google Analytics to track anonymized website usage. Google Analytics does not collect personally identifying information. Review Google's privacy policy for more information.
  • Hosting Provider: Our website is hosted on a secure server. Your data in transit is protected by SSL encryption.

Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses cookies to enhance your experience:

  • Essential cookies: These are necessary for the website to function properly (e.g., remembering login sessions).
  • Analytics cookies: Google Analytics uses cookies to track anonymous usage patterns. These cookies do not contain personal information.
  • Preference cookies: We may use cookies to remember your display preferences and settings.

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through your browser settings. You can decline analytics cookies without affecting website functionality. If you wish to opt out of Google Analytics entirely, you can use Google's opt-out browser extension.

Data Retention

We keep your personal information only as long as necessary:

  • Form submissions: We retain contact and inquiry information for up to 3 years to follow up on potential projects and for record-keeping purposes.
  • Meeting information: Calendly bookings are retained according to your calendar management needs; we do not separately retain this data beyond your own records.
  • Analytics data: Google Analytics data is automatically aggregated and anonymized. We retain general analytics reports for up to 2 years for business analysis.
  • Email communications: We may retain email correspondence related to projects or inquiries for business and legal purposes.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to access: You can request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to correction: You can request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Right to deletion: You can request that we delete your personal information, subject to certain legal obligations.
  • Right to opt-out: You can opt out of marketing communications at any time by replying to emails with your request or contacting us directly.

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information in the "Contact Us" section below. We will respond to reasonable requests within 30 days.

Data Security

We take the security of your personal information seriously. We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including:

  • SSL encryption for data transmitted between your browser and our website
  • Secure, password-protected access to our systems
  • Regular reviews of our security practices
  • Limitations on who within our organization can access personal data

While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we are committed to safeguarding your data.

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without parental consent, we will promptly delete that information. Please contact us if you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

Muntin Digital™ may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Effective" date at the top of this policy. Your continued use of our website following the posting of changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
