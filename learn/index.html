Plain-English glossary

Every
technical term, translated.

Forty-plus web, SEO, and restaurant-ops terms, written the way a friend would explain them over coffee. If you've ever nodded along when someone said "schema" or "Core Web Vitals," start here.

Open the glossary

  • Core Web Vitals

    Three numbers Google uses to decide if your site feels fast. Miss one badly and your search ranking takes a visible hit.

  • Schema

    Invisible markup that tells Google your menu is a menu and your hours are hours. Missing schema is why some restaurants never appear in the map pack.

  • Google Business Profile

    The free listing that shows up on the right side of Google. For a local restaurant, it's usually worth more than the website itself.

  • Mobile-first indexing

    Google reads your phone version, not your desktop one. If the mobile experience is worse, your whole site is worse in Google's eyes.
