      What this dashboard isn't.

      Not your books. Cost Pulse only sees the invoices you've actively saved through the Invoice Decoder. It won't catch a forgotten invoice, won't sync to QuickBooks or Restaurant365, and won't reconcile against your bank.

      Not benchmarked. The trends you see here are your data only. We don't pool data across operators, don't sell aggregated cost benchmarks, and don't backfill historical invoices into a future opt-in even if one ships.

      vs Restaurant365 / MarginEdge: their dashboards live in their cloud and read everything you upload. Cost Pulse reads only what you've actively saved here, all encrypted with a key derived from your sign-in. The math is local.
