    2. 
      
  2. No account, no email. The page works without identifying you in any way.
    3. 
      
  3. No browser memory. Closing the tab clears everything. The form is empty when you reopen the URL.

    5. 
      
  5. Privacy-safe analytics. Plausible counts how many people use the tool, but never sees your hours, your restaurant name, or your city. Only enum-bucketed values like “4-5 days open” pass through.
