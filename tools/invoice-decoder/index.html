    What this is, and what it isn't.

    Is: a fast way to turn one invoice photo, scan, or PDF into sorted line items the operator owns. Encrypted at rest, tied only to your sign-in. Categorized into restaurant-real groups (protein, produce, dairy, dry goods, paper, cleaning, beverage, herbs-spices).

    Isn't: your books. Won't sync to QuickBooks, Restaurant365, or Toast. Won't catch every pricing error — treat the sanity check as a second pair of eyes, not an auditor. Plate costs only update on tap-confirm.

    vs. Restaurant365: your invoices stay on your device until you explicitly save. Saved invoices are encrypted with a key derived from your sign-in. No benchmark dataset, no vendor pool, no ML training on your cost data — and never backfilled retroactively if a future opt-in arrives.

    Privacy posture: Open DevTools → Network. Pick a photo. Cleaned-up version renders in this page. Network panel stays empty. Image bytes never leave your device.
