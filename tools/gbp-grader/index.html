What's a Google Business Profile (GBP)?

It's the panel that shows up on Google when someone searches your name — the box on the right with your rating, hours, photos, and "Order online" / "Reserve" buttons. It's free, you control it, and on mobile it usually decides whether someone walks in. If you've never seen yours, search "[your restaurant name] [your city]" on Google right now.
