How we protect the numbers you type

The calculators above don't exist yet — this page is still being built. When each one lands, it will ship with a numbered, inspectable list of the steps we take to keep your inputs out of our hands. Every claim will be verifiable in your own browser in under a minute; we'll show you how to check us.

You can start verifying right now: open DevTools → Network tab and reload this page. You'll see a handful of same-origin requests plus one call to plausible.io (analytics, with a strict allow-list of event properties — no raw numbers, ever). Nothing else.
