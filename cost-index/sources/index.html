The public sources

The index builds only on citable public data — credited by agency and report identifier, never a private survey or a phoned-in assessment.

  • USDA Livestock, Poultry & Grain Market News — mandatory, transaction-reported wholesale prices for beef, pork, and poultry. ams.usda.gov
  • USDA AMS terminal markets — daily produce wholesale prices, sampled across regional terminal markets. ams.usda.gov
  • U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (PPI) — producer price indexes for food commodities. bls.gov
  • FRED, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis — national average price series. fred.stlouisfed.org
  • U.S. Energy Information Administration — diesel and electricity, the upstream cost drivers. eia.gov
  • NOAA Fisheries — seafood landings and prices. fisheries.noaa.gov
