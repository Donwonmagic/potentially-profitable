Why it's moving

Why it matters

Watermelon trades per carton as a wholesale reference. That is the market's figure, not the delivered price at your door — your invoice adds freight, the vendor's margin, and your volume. Always compare in the same unit.

The price moves with forces upstream of your kitchen — chiefly diesel. That is an association, not a direct cause, but when those climb, watermelon tends to follow. So a higher invoice is not always the vendor's doing: sometimes the whole market moved.
