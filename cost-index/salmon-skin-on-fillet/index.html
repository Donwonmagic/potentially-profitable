Salmon (skin-on fillet)

Coverage in progress

We track salmon (skin-on fillet) for the index, but we don't yet have a complete, free wholesale read we'd stand behind — so we're not publishing a number. The index shows a price only when public data supports an honest one.

Why there's no number yet

The rule is simple: a price ships only when we can source it from public USDA, BLS or FRED data at a quality we'd act on ourselves. For salmon (skin-on fillet), the free wholesale series we need isn't wired up yet — and a thin, single-source guess would be worse than nothing.

What we do know

Even without a live price for salmon (skin-on fillet) yet, we do know its edible-portion yield: ~80%. About 80% of what you buy reaches the plate after trim and waste (~20% loss), so your true cost per usable pound runs higher than the purchase price.
