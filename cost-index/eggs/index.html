Dairy & eggs

Eggs

Coverage in progress

We track eggs for the index, but we don't yet have a complete, free wholesale read we'd stand behind — so we're not publishing a number. The index shows a price only when public data supports an honest one.

Why there's no number yet

The rule is simple: a price ships only when we can source it from public USDA, BLS or FRED data at a quality we'd act on ourselves. For eggs, the free wholesale series we need isn't wired up yet — and a thin, single-source guess would be worse than nothing.

What you can do now

Check your last eggs invoice against your own recent ones, or open Cost Pulse for the ingredients we do cover. This page fills in when the data does.
