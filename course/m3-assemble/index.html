Module 3 of 4 · Lessons 8–11 of 16 · About 2 hours total

Assemble — the operator paperwork that decides whether you fill tables.

Menu, photos, hours, Google Business Profile. Each one of these is unglamorous on its own and load-bearing in combination. By the end of Module 3, your home page has its actual menu, your photos brief is in hand, your hours are set, and your GBP either exists or is queued for repair.

All six lessons of Module 3 shipped. Both tracks now complete through to Module 4.

Lessons in this module

8

Your menu shortlist

~30 minSharedShipped

A short shortlist of dishes that anchors your menu page. Drag-rank them, set prices, link out to plate-cost analysis if you want to verify margins. The menu is the marketing.
9

Photos — by track

~25 minForksShipped

Fresh-track operators rank the five shot types every restaurant site needs and write a brief for the first three. Rebuild operators triage existing photos into keep / refresh first / quietly kill.

Fresh track 9a · Photo brief from scratch

Drag-rank eight shot types; write specific notes for the top three.

 Rebuild track 9b · Photo refresh triage

Three-bucket sort (keep / refresh / kill); rank what to refresh first.
10

Hours, holidays, contact

~20 minSharedShipped

Set your weekly hours day-by-day, mark closures, write your phone number once. The most-read piece of your home page, on every device.
11

Google Business Profile — by track

~30 minForksShipped

Fresh-track operators claim, fill the twelve fields, and write the 750-character description in their own voice. Rebuild operators rank ten common GBP problems and write a punch list for the next two weeks.

Fresh track 11a · GBP from zero

Claim, the twelve fields, write the description, ship.

 Rebuild track 11b · GBP repair

Rank ten common problems; write top-three fixes; name an owner.

What you have when Module 3 is done

Your home page is no longer mostly placeholders — it has your menu, your hours, your phone, your photos (or a brief), and your GBP is in shape. Module 4 wraps with local SEO, reviews, the L14 generator, deploy, and the 30-day rhythm.
