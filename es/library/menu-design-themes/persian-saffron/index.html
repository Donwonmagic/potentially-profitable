Historia del tema

Saffron-cream paper, indigo accent, eight-pointed star (girih / khatam) motif. A theme operators of high-end Persian restaurants asked for; previously they were defaulting to brasserie or modern-minimal which miss the cultural register.

Inspirado en

  • Persian / Iranian dining rooms
  • Modern Iranian-American restaurants in DC and Bethesda
