Explainer · May 23, 2026 · 6 min read · By Don Goldstein

Gemini quietly became your #2 referral source. It reads your Google profile.

ChatGPT still sends the most AI referral traffic, but its share is falling while Gemini's climbs fast. For a restaurant, Gemini is the more winnable of the two — because it reads the Google profile you already manage.

In short

  • ChatGPT still sends the most AI referral traffic but its share is falling (89.2% to 81.4%); Gemini rose from 4.3% to 11.6% in Q1 2026 and 13.2% by April.
  • Gemini is the most winnable AI source for a restaurant because it reads Google's own ecosystem — your profile, Maps, and reviews.
  • Keep the Google Business Profile complete and current and it feeds three surfaces at once: the map pack, the AI Overview, and Gemini.

4.3% to 13.2% in a single quarter. That is Gemini’s share of AI referral traffic, roughly tripling between January and April 2026 — while ChatGPT, still the leader, slid from 89.2% to 81.4% over the same stretch. Two numbers, two directions. The short version for a restaurant: Gemini is now the clear #2 AI referral source, it is bigger than every other non-ChatGPT assistant combined, and it is the more winnable of the two — because it reads the Google profile you already manage. The rest of this piece is the data behind that, and what to do with it this week.

Source: BrightEdge, May 2026

BrightEdge — AI referral-traffic data (released May 20, 2026): Gemini 4.3% → 11.6% in Q1, 13.2% in April; larger than Perplexity, Claude, Meta AI, DeepSeek, and Grok combined.

Figures are BrightEdge’s reported shares of AI-assistant referral traffic across its dataset. Shares move month to month; the durable point is the climb to a clear second place.

Same dataset, opposite arrows: ChatGPT sliding from 89.2% toward 81.4%, Gemini climbing from 4.3% past 13.2%. You do not have to pick the winner — you have to notice that the riser is the one whose ranking surface is already on your to-do list.

Why Gemini is the winnable one

Here is the part that matters for a restaurant: Gemini reads Google’s own ecosystem. When it answers “good ramen near me,” it leans on your Google Business Profile, your Maps presence, and your reviews — the exact assets you already manage. ChatGPT and Perplexity pull from a wider, messier web you have far less control over. So the same profile work that helps you show up in a Google AI Overview also feeds Gemini. You are not optimizing for a new platform; you are getting paid twice for the one you already work on.

The number that makes Gemini worth the attention

At 13.2% of AI referrals, Gemini is bigger than every other non-ChatGPT assistant combined — Perplexity, Claude, Meta AI, DeepSeek, and Grok, added together, still come up short. So if you are going to learn one AI surface after ChatGPT, the math says learn the one that reads your Google profile. Per the BrightEdge data above.

How to see it — and earn more of it

First, look. In your analytics, AI assistants show up as referrals from hosts like gemini.google.com and chatgpt.com; if you have never checked, that is the number to start watching. The Search Console walkthrough shows where to find the search side of it.

Then earn it the cheap way: keep the Google profile complete and current, because for Gemini that profile is the ranking surface. Run the GBP grader for the gaps, the SEO grader for the on-site signals that back the profile up, and search ideas for the questions worth answering in text. The setup basics, if the profile is new, are in setting up your Google Business Profile.

The reason the same hour of work pays off so differently across the two leaders comes down to where each one reads from. Put them side by side:

ChatGPT (81.4%, sliding) reads the open web A wide, messy index you have little direct control over — scraped pages, aggregator listings, whatever it crawled. You can influence it, but you cannot hand it a single clean file and know it will be read.
Gemini (13.2%, climbing) reads your Google profile Your Business Profile, your Maps listing, your reviews — the exact assets you already maintain. The same file the map pack reads. Fill it once, correctly, and three surfaces quote it: the map pack, the AI Overview, and the fastest-growing AI referral source you have.

From the floor

I started watching the referrer line in our analytics the way I watch a reservation book — a quick glance, same time each week. gemini.google.com was not even a row a year ago; now it sits just under chatgpt.com and climbs most weeks. What struck me on the floor is how little new work it asked for. The hours, the cuisine tags, the menu link I keep current so a guest can find us on Maps — that is the same file Gemini reads before it names anyone. I was already doing the work; I just was not getting credit for it on a surface I had not thought to check.

What to do this week

The practical move is not “learn Gemini.” It is to keep the Google profile complete and current — because that one profile is now feeding three surfaces at once: the map pack, the AI Overview, and the fastest-growing AI referral source you have. Three small moves, in order:

Key takeaways

  • Gemini is the clear #2 AI referral source, bigger than every other non-ChatGPT assistant combined.
  • The same Google Business Profile work pays twice — for Google's AI Overview and for Gemini.
  • AI assistants show up in analytics as referrals from hosts like gemini.google.com; start watching that number.
  • Optimizing for Gemini is mostly optimizing the Google profile you already manage.
