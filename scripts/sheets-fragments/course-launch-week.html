Days 3-4 — Reconcile the old listings Every diner who Googled your restaurant in the last year landed on whatever URL Google had cached. If you don't update these surfaces, half your traffic clicks into a dead address.
TaskDone?DateNotes / URL
5. Set up 301 redirects from old URL → new URL (if migrating)
6. Update website URL field in your Google Business Profile
7. Update website URL on Yelp, TripAdvisor, OpenTable, Resy, Toast, DoorDash, Uber Eats — wherever you're listed
Days 5-7 — Put the URL where diners see it The site doesn't exist until people know about it. Three concrete places + a review-collection prompt.
TaskDone?DateNotes / URL
8. Add the URL to your Instagram bio + Facebook page + LinkedIn
9. Print 100 small cards / stickers with the URL; place at host stand, in takeout bags, at register
10. Email 10 regulars: "we have a new site, would you tell us if anything is broken?" — most will leave a review at the same time
