Off Day Collective.
Silence, curated.

A narrative site for a lifestyle brand positioning rest as a ritual practice. Cormorant Garamond and Inter, cream-and-gold on warm near-black, a three-tempo phase system, and copy written like the back of a quietly confident book.

Project overview

Brand strategy
before pixels.

This is what a lifestyle brand built around intentional rest looks like as a design and strategy exercise — voice, information architecture, typography, color system, and copy cadence, all thought through before anyone touches a layout tool. The site is now live at offdaycollective.com; the case study below is what went on the whiteboard before any of it existed.

The challenge

Teach a new category, not sell a product

Nobody is searching for "off day rituals" yet — the category doesn't exist. The site has to create the vocabulary, justify the premise, and earn the email address, all before the shop opens. That's a narrative site, not a product page.

The approach

A single-page cinematic experience

CSS scroll snapping, parallax typography, expanding product tiles, and a dark editorial palette that treats the visitor like a reader, not a conversion target. Fully vanilla HTML/CSS/JS for sub-second load and accessibility from the first frame.

The outcome

A brand site that reads like a book

Three daily tempos (Morning, Journey, Evening), a library of free peer-reviewed reading, a philosophy section, and a single email capture at the end. Every element earns its place by advancing the argument for intentional rest.

Brand palette

Warm darks.
Muted gold. No noise.

The palette avoids every saturated or cool tone on the wheel. Cream and parchment carry the light surfaces. Walnut, charcoal, and warm near-black carry the dark surfaces. Muted gold is the only accent — the same color as late-afternoon light through a window.

Typography

Cormorant Garamond
and Inter.

A tightly tracked Cormorant Garamond in weight 300 italic carries every display line — a letterform that whispers instead of declares. Inter at the same weight handles the body, labels, and clinical copy. The pairing reads like an essay, not an ad.

Display · Cormorant Garamond · 300 Italic

Silence is a luxury.

Body · Inter · 300

That person standing still while the world moves around them? That's who you are on your best off day. We're building everything you need to get there — curated objects, guided rituals, and peer-reviewed reading, sold as a single system.

Information architecture

Three tempos.
Three states of mind.

The site breaks the day into three phases, each with its own accent color, its own ritual, and its own library article. Every product, practice, and reading is filed under one of them. Visitors don't browse a store — they walk through a day.

Phase I · Clarity

Morning Stillness

Protecting the first ninety minutes post-waking from your inbox, your newsfeed, and your own restlessness. Rituals built around the cortisol awakening response, warm drinks, and analog tools.

06:00 – 08:30

Phase II · Momentum

Day's Journey

Midday movement, creative idling, and the hours where the off day actually earns its name. Guided practices that pair walking, reading, and staring at nothing on purpose.

10:00 – 16:00

Phase III · Reflection

Evening Release

A wind-down practice grounded in sleep science — glymphatic recovery, circadian lighting, and the rituals that close the day instead of just ending it.

19:00 – 22:30
Voice

Every sentence
earns its place.

The copy was written like a magazine pull-quote wall — short, declarative, warm enough to trust, confident enough to earn the email. Here are the lines that anchor the whole site.

Signature phrases · Cormorant Garamond · 300 italic

Silence is a luxury. The modern world is weightless. We curate gravity. Rest is productive. Science you can feel — and take to your doctor. Curated objects. Guided rituals. Weekends that restore.
