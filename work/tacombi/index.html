What the work looked like

Marketing a taqueria
from the inside.

Tacombi is a NYC-born taqueria that expanded into the DMV with locations in Bethesda and Arlington. As lead of marketing and community engagement, I designed and executed the campaigns that brought neighbors through the door — not from a desk at an agency, but from behind the counter where I could see whether the work was actually landing.

The context

Two new DMV locations needed local roots

Tacombi's brand was built in New York. Opening in Bethesda and Arlington meant earning trust in neighborhoods that had never heard of the brand — through hyperlocal campaigns, not national advertising.

The approach

Neighborhood-first marketing

Discount postcards mailed to surrounding blocks. Cross-promotions with Landmark Theaters next door. A loyalty program (Taco Taco Club) that rewarded repeat visits. QR-coded collateral that drove reviews and app signups.

The range

Print, social, seasonal, loyalty

Happy hour posters, holiday hosting campaigns, bilingual receipt cards, review-generation programs, and location-specific neighborhood discount mailers — all designed, printed, and distributed in-house.
