A productized offer

The $499 Restaurant Website Audit.

A deep read of your existing site by someone who runs restaurants. 90-minute Loom walkthrough plus an 8-page action sheet, in writing, in five business days. Flat fee. No retainer, no upsell.

$499flat · 5 business days

What you get

  • The 90-minute Loom. I open your site on my phone, on my laptop, and from a Google search for your name. You watch what I see — the broken hours block at the top of mobile, the menu that takes 14 seconds to render, the schema that’s missing, the photo that’s 4 MB. Voice-over the whole way. Yours forever.
  • The 8-page action sheet. A PDF you can print and pin in the office. Three columns: what’s broken, why it costs you, what to do this month. Ranked by dollar impact, not by how clever the fix is.
  • The reservations leak number. An estimate, in dollars, of how much your current site costs you per month in lost reservations and direct orders. The math is shown; the inputs are yours.
  • Vendor-by-vendor swap recommendations. If your reservations system, ordering plugin, or photo host is the actual problem, you’ll know which to keep, which to change, and what each move would cost you in time and money.
  • One follow-up email. 30 days after delivery, I check in: which fixes did you ship? Which didn’t make it? What’s blocking you? No charge, no obligation.

What it is not

  • A redesign. The action sheet recommends fixes for the site you already have.
  • A sales call. The deliverables are the deliverables. There’s no second meeting where I pitch a build.
  • A template report. The Loom is recorded live for your site only. I do not run an audit on the same day for two restaurants.
  • Generic. If the bottleneck is your photography rather than your code, the report says so — and recommends a photographer.

Credit toward a build

If you decide to hire the studio for an Essentials, Full Service, or Restaurant Group build within 60 days of the audit delivery, the $499 comes off the build invoice. The audit doubles as the discovery call you’d be paying for anyway, and you keep the action sheet either way.

How to start

  1. Send a one-line note through The Window with your URL. Same business day, always inside two.
  2. I confirm the slot, send a Stripe invoice. Pay before delivery, no deposit games.
  3. Five business days later, the Loom and the PDF arrive in your inbox.
  4. Thirty days after that, I check in.
