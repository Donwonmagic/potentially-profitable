A productized offer

The $249 Menu Polish.

You drafted the menu yourself — in the free Menu Design Suite, in InDesign, in Word, on a napkin you photographed in good light. It’s 80% there. The last 20% is what a designer sees that you don’t: a kerning that’s a hair off, a hierarchy that fights itself, a section break that breaks the eye. Three business days. Press-ready PDF/X-3 back. $249 flat.

Take what you built and refine it. No DIY tool. No AI guesswork. Hand-touched, by one person, in writing.

$249flat · 3 business days

What you get

  • A press-ready PDF/X-3 of your menu, exported to ISO-coated CMYK with embedded fonts and bleed marks. Hand it to any commercial printer in the country and they’ll know exactly what to do.
  • A web-friendly JPG and PNG, color-managed and right-sized, for the website and the GBP photo slot.
  • The InDesign source. Yours to keep. If your nephew takes the next round in-house, he has the same starting point I did.
  • Typography refined. Type pair tightened, sizes balanced, leading set against a real grid. Section heads aligned to the eye, not just to the math.
  • Layout balanced. Whitespace where the menu earns it; density where the menu can carry it. Item descriptions trimmed (with your sign-off) so the page doesn’t fight itself.
  • EN+ES typography pass. If your menu carries Spanish copy, both columns get the same care — widow control, accent kerning, and the tilde isn’t orphaned at the end of a line.

Who it’s for

  • You drafted a menu and it’s 80% there. You can feel the 20% but can’t name it.
  • You’re printing this week and you don’t want to walk into the printer’s with a Word doc.
  • You’re bilingual and the Spanish column never quite sits right next to the English one.
  • You don’t need a full $1,500 Menu Drop-In — the design is yours, you just want a designer to clean it up before it goes out.

What I accept as input

The free Menu Design Suite exports a clean draft and that’s the smoothest path. But I’ll work from whatever you have:

  • Menu Design Suite export (.indd, .pdf, or shareable link)
  • Word document or Google Doc
  • Existing PDF (even one made from a Word doc)
  • A clear photo of a printed menu (good light, lay flat, no glare)
  • InDesign or Affinity Publisher source files

If what you send is too far from a starting point — a pile of dish names with no structure, a scan I can’t read — I’ll refund the $249 the same day and tell you why a Menu Drop-In is the better fit.

The upgrade ramp

If within 60 days you decide you want me to build the whole menu page on your live site, the $249 carries forward — you pay the $1,251 difference and I ship the full $1,500 Menu Drop-In. No re-quote, no re-discovery. Same operator, same week.

How it works

  1. Day zero. You send the draft (any of the formats above) and a one-paragraph note about your brand voice and what’s bothering you about the menu. Stripe invoice goes out same day; payment in full before work starts.
  2. Day 1. I read the menu the way an operator reads it — on a phone, then on paper, then aloud. Notes back to you if anything trips me.
  3. Day 2. Typography pass, layout pass, color check. ES column reviewed by a fluent Spanish reader if your menu carries one.
  4. Day 3. Press-ready PDF/X-3 + web JPG/PNG + InDesign source delivered. One round of edits inside the same week if something looks off.

Capacity & refunds

  • Six polishes a week, one operator. The waitlist is real. If this week is booked, you’ll see “join the waitlist” instead of “buy — $249,” and the next open week is named on the page.
  • Refund policy. Full refund any time before you submit the intake. Once intake is in for 24 hours, we’re at the press — the work is underway and the fee is final.
