A productized offer · between DIY and Drop-In

The $249 Menu Polish.

You typed your dishes into our free Menu Design Suite. The layout’s 90% there. Three business days from now, it’s a press-ready PDF the print shop can run with no questions: typography balanced, hierarchy tightened, kerning fixed, bleed and crop marks set, fonts subset for clean output. Flat fee, one revision, you keep the source.

$249flat · 3 business days

What you get

  • Press-ready PDF/X-3 with 3mm bleed, hairline crop marks, and an OutputIntents profile your printer will recognize. Drop it in their portal; no back-and-forth.
  • Typography pass. Kerning on display headings, leading inside dish blocks, leader-dot weight on prices, optical alignment on numbers. The differences a designer sees and a diner feels.
  • Layout balance. Section breaks where the eye expects them; orphan-and-widow cleanup on long descriptions; price column alignment that holds across paper sizes.
  • Allergen footer that complies. Auto-disclaimer text matched to your regime (FDA Big 9, EU FIC 14, UK PPDS, Canada, AU/NZ); allergen key emitted only for codes actually used; bilingual when your menu is bilingual.
  • One revision. Send notes; we ship the v2 within 24 hours. Anything bigger gets quoted, but small touch-ups are part of the deal.
  • Source files. The InDesign source comes with your PDF. Your future-self or your future-designer can pick up where we left off — no vendor lock-in.

Who it’s for

  • You’ve used the free Menu Design Suite. The PDF that comes out is good. You want it to be obviously professional.
  • You’ve been quoted $1,500–$3,000 for “just polish my menu” and that math doesn’t fit this season.
  • You’re reprinting for a season change, a price update, or an allergen-regime shift — and you want this version to be the one you keep using.
  • You serve a bilingual community and your menu needs to ship in EN and ES with the same care.

The upgrade ramp

If within 60 days you decide you want me to take this further — a full custom menu page on your existing site, in your brand, EN/ES, schema-marked — the $249 credits toward the $1,500 Menu Drop-In. You pay the $1,251 difference. No re-quote, no re-discovery; we already know your menu.

How it works

  1. Day zero. Click the button below. The form pre-fills with your dishes, theme, palette, and paper size from the tool — you don’t retype anything. Stripe invoice goes out same day; payment in full before work starts.
  2. Day 1. I open your brief, set the typography pass, and send a 60-second Loom showing the before-and-after.
  3. Day 2. You see a proof PDF in your inbox. One round of edits.
  4. Day 3. Press-ready PDF/X-3 + InDesign source delivered. You’re at the printer the same afternoon.
