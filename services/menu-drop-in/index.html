A productized offer

The $1,500 Menu Drop-In.

Most restaurant websites have one page that’s costing them dinners: the menu. We’ll build a single custom HTML menu page — mobile-fast, properly marked up, EN/ES — and plug it into your existing Wix, Squarespace, or Shopify site without the rebuild. Seven business days, flat fee.

$1,500flat · 7 business days

What you get

  • One custom menu page, built to your brand. Hand-coded HTML/CSS, no template fight, no app dependency. Loads under 1.5 seconds on a phone on 4G.
  • Bilingual. EN and ES versions, both indexed, hreflang-stamped. The Spanish version isn’t a Google Translate plugin — it’s a real localized page reviewed by a fluent Spanish-speaking writer.
  • Schema markup. Restaurant + Menu + MenuItem JSON-LD so Google and Apple Maps see your dishes and prices. Validated with Google’s Rich Results Test before delivery.
  • Mobile-first layout. Sticky category nav. One-tap call/directions/reservation. Photos optimized to AVIF + WebP fallbacks. Tested on iPhone SE up to iPad.
  • Drop-in instructions. A short Loom for whoever maintains your existing site, showing exactly where to paste the embed snippet (Wix, Squarespace, Shopify, or self-hosted). No CMS access required.
  • Two free updates in the first 90 days. Menu changes, price corrections, holiday-hour swap-ins. Anything bigger gets quoted, but small touch-ups are part of the deal.

Who it’s for

  • You like the rest of your site. The bones, the homepage, the about, the photos — they work. The menu is the one that drags.
  • You can’t justify $5k+ for a full rebuild this quarter, but you can see the menu page is the leak.
  • You serve a bilingual community and your current site only ships English.
  • You’ve been using a menu PDF that nobody on a phone can read.

The upgrade ramp

If within 90 days you decide the menu drop-in went well and want me to take on the full site, the $1,500 credits toward an Essentials build — you pay the $1,000 difference and I rebuild the rest of the site against the new menu page. No re-quote, no re-discovery.

How it works

  1. Day zero. You send your menu (PDF, doc, photos, whatever you have) and the URL of your existing site. Stripe invoice goes out same day; payment in full before work starts.
  2. Days 1–3. I draft the page in your brand. You see a staging URL on day 3.
  3. Days 4–5. One round of edits. Two if the second round is small.
  4. Days 6–7. Schema validated, photos optimized, ES translation reviewed by a native speaker, embed snippet packaged.
  5. Day 7. Loom + embed snippet sent. Your site builder pastes it in, or I do, depending on platform.
