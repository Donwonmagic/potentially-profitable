What’s included, every month.

Nine line items, every one of them a thing that costs more not to do than to do. The whole thing fits inside two hours of my attention every month, by design.

  • Hosting

    Cloudflare Pages or your existing host, with the SSL renewal monitored. Five-nines uptime, no theatre.

  • Daily backups

    Site + database snapshotted nightly to a separate region. 30-day rolling restore window.

  • Hours hygiene

    Once a month I check that the hours on your site, GBP, Yelp, and Apple Maps match. Holiday hours flagged 14 days out.

  • Menu price audit

    Spot-check that your published prices match the kitchen’s. Mismatches surfaced in your monthly note.

  • Menu refresh, quarterly

    Once a quarter I take your menu changes — price bumps, item swaps, seasonal ingredients — and hand-build a fresh press-ready PDF. Bundled in.

  • Uptime monitoring

    UptimeRobot probes your homepage and contact form every five minutes. If anything breaks at 2am, I see it before you do.

  • SSL + domain renewal

    Reminders 60 days out, paid by you, set up in your registrar account. No domain held hostage.

  • One small request per month

    A new menu line item, a Thanksgiving hours swap, a press-mention added to the homepage. Up to 30 minutes; bigger asks get quoted.

  • Monthly note

    One email, second Friday of the month: what I checked, what I changed, what to keep an eye on. Two minutes to read.

The monthly cadence, calendared.

A month at Care Plan Light, predictable enough that the kitchen can ignore it. Predictable on purpose.

Week 1

The hygiene pass.

I run the hours-and-prices check across your site, GBP, Yelp, and Apple Maps. Drift flagged, fixes pushed.

Week 2

The Friday note.

Second Friday. What I checked, what I changed, what to watch. Two minutes to read.

Weeks 3–4

Your one small request.

A new menu line, a press mention, a holiday hours swap. Up to 30 minutes — reply to the note and it’s queued.

All month

Uptime + SSL watch.

Every 5 minutes a probe checks your homepage + contact form. SSL alert fires 60 days before expiry.

Who it’s for.

Your site works fine. You’d just rather not be the one remembering to update the holiday hours in four places.
You’ve had one too many one-star reviews from guests who showed up when Yelp said you were open.
You just had me ship an Essentials build or a Menu Drop-In, and you want me to keep the surface current.
You don’t need real-time on-call (yet). One small request a month and a calm Friday email is enough.

What it is not.

In writing, before you click subscribe

  • Not a redesign retainer. Layout changes, new pages, photo replacements, brand work — those get quoted separately. Care Plan Light is hygiene, not new construction.
  • Not 24/7 white-glove. If your site breaks at 2am Sunday, I see the alert and fix it Monday morning. For real-time on-call, look at the full Care Plan ($225/mo).
  • Not for sites I didn’t build. The plan only covers sites the studio shipped, or sites we’ve done a takeover on (which is a separate one-time engagement starting at $750).

Cancel any time. No call required to start or stop.

Bookable without a sales call.

Send a one-line note with your URL through The Window. I confirm fit within four business hours. If your site isn’t one I can care for, I’ll tell you straight — no upsell, no callback funnel.

Cancel from your dashboard, not a phone call.

Stripe handles the monthly $99. Cancel any time, any reason, no questions, no “just one more month” rep call. The 30-day backup window stays accessible for 30 days after cancellation.
